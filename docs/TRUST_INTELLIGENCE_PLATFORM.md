# Trust Intelligence Platform

Cyber Sentinels is the operational Trust Intelligence layer for regulated enterprise workflows. It connects humans, AI agents, machine identities, credentials, evidence, replay and governance into one explainable trust record.

It is not an AI detector. Detection can be one signal, but Cyber Sentinels does not turn a provider result, heuristic result or model output into an unsupported truth claim. Real ML, Provider API, Heuristic Baseline, Awaiting Credentials and Not Implemented remain separate source states.

It is not an IAM platform. IAM systems grant and enforce access. Cyber Sentinels records the authority lineage around consequential work: who or what acted, under whose authority, what evidence existed, what changed and whether governance review restored, reduced or escalated trust.

It is not a SIEM. SIEM tools aggregate security events. Cyber Sentinels focuses on workflow trust: evidence continuity, replayable outcomes, reviewer actions, authorization context, trust posture and operational memory for regulated decisions.

It is not an EDR. EDR tools monitor endpoints and respond to device compromise. Cyber Sentinels governs trust across workflow actors, AI agents, credentials, documents, media, sessions and provider evidence without claiming endpoint detection or autonomous remediation.

Instead, Cyber Sentinels provides:

- Runtime trust execution that evaluates signals without blocking every workflow on external providers.
- Normalized evidence that preserves source, confidence, timestamp, replay reference and trust engine reference.
- Provider independence through a single abstraction layer for status, confidence, latency, limitations and normalized evidence.
- Continuous Trust Posture that can increase, decrease, decay, recover, require step-up authentication or be restored by governance.
- A trust graph that explains why a workflow is trusted by linking human authority, AI agents, machine identities, credentials, workflow evidence, replay, governance and posture.
- Validation discipline that tracks datasets, reviewed outcomes, provider comparison, benchmark versions, calibration history and confidence drift without fabricating metrics.
- Enterprise sovereignty over trust memory, restricted data, provider policy and workflow evidence.

The platform promise is simple: make operational trust explainable, governed and replayable without replacing accountable human authority or existing systems of record.
