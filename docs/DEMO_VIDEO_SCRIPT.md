# Cyber Sentinels 90-Second Demo Video Script

## Purpose

Show an enterprise audience how Cyber Sentinels turns a suspicious hiring session into an explainable, human-governed operational record. Keep the screen calm, the cursor deliberate and the story moving.

## Voiceover and screen direction

### 0:00-0:10 — AI changed trust

**Screen:** Homepage. Hold on the hiring-security headline, then select **View Demo**.

**Voiceover:** "AI changed what organizations can create. It also changed what they can trust. A convincing identity, voice or video feed is no longer enough to establish who — or what — is entering a critical workflow."

### 0:10-0:20 — A synthetic candidate enters

**Screen:** `/demo`, then open **Hiring Attack**. Advance to **Synthetic candidate enters**.

**Voiceover:** "Here, a synthetic candidate enters a remote hiring workflow with a credible profile but incomplete verification evidence. Cyber Sentinels opens a verification workflow instead of treating appearance as proof."

### 0:20-0:34 — Session integrity flags injection risk

**Screen:** Advance through verification and session checks to **Injection risk detected**. Briefly show `/demo/session-integrity`.

**Voiceover:** "Identity is only the entry state. During the interview, session-integrity checks keep liveness, media risk and channel evidence separate. An injection-risk signal indicates that the live feed may no longer represent the verified context."

### 0:34-0:47 — Governance review opens

**Screen:** Return to the hiring flow. Advance to **Governance review opened**, then show the active flag and review state.

**Voiceover:** "The signal does not make a hiring decision. It pauses the workflow and opens an accountable governance review, with evidence, policy context and a named human owner."

### 0:47-0:59 — Audit trail created

**Screen:** Advance to **Audit trail generated**, then open the replay chronology.

**Voiceover:** "Every material transition is recorded. The replay timeline reconstructs verification events, session changes, evidence history and reviewer actions in order."

### 0:59-1:10 — Threat blocked

**Screen:** Advance through **Manual review escalated** to **Threat blocked**.

**Voiceover:** "The reviewer examines the evidence and blocks the compromised session. The threat is contained without turning an automated risk signal into a judgment about the candidate."

### 1:10-1:22 — Verification receipt issued

**Screen:** Open the enterprise verification receipt. Move slowly across identity, session integrity, deepfake risk, injection risk, governance outcome and audit references.

**Voiceover:** "A verification receipt preserves what was checked, what changed, who acted and why. Security, talent and compliance teams now share the same portable, audit-ready explanation."

### 1:22-1:30 — Close

**Screen:** Return to the Cyber Sentinels wordmark or homepage. End on a clean black frame with the closing line.

**Voiceover:** "The future of AI is not only intelligence. It is trust."

## Recording notes

- Use sample-only data and hide browser bookmarks, personal accounts and environment details.
- Keep zoom at a readable level and avoid rapid cursor movement.
- Do not imply perfect detection, automated candidate rejection or continuous surveillance.
- If a data-backed replay is unavailable, use the interactive demo and describe replay rather than improvising unsupported results.
- Target 85 seconds in rehearsal to leave room for transitions.
