# Synthetic Trust Market Alignment

Cyber Sentinels is positioned as the enterprise workflow trust layer for decision-layer trust defense. The platform focus is not only whether media is synthetic. It is whether a person, session, workflow, reviewer action and evidence chain can still be trusted when a critical enterprise decision is about to move forward.

## Synthetic Trust Attacks

Synthetic credibility, not just synthetic media, is becoming the attack surface.

Attackers can combine plausible identities, polished profiles, generated answers, proxy participation, manipulated meeting channels and automated workflows to look credible inside normal enterprise processes. The risk is not limited to a deepfake image or video. It is the full decision context around whether a workflow should be trusted.

Cyber Sentinels treats these events as reviewable workflow trust problems:

- synthetic applicants entering hiring funnels
- proxy interviews and mismatched candidate context
- AI-assisted interview fraud and coached responses
- session integrity anomalies across channel, device, voice or video context
- governance escalation when evidence, state or reviewer action changes

The platform does not claim perfect detection. It preserves reviewable evidence, flags integrity anomalies, records governance escalation and keeps humans accountable for sensitive decisions.

## Decision-Layer Trust Defense

Cyber Sentinels protects the decision layer: the workflow, evidence, reviewer action and trust state behind critical enterprise decisions.

This means the product connects:

- workflow trust state
- identity and session integrity context
- verification evidence
- governance review
- reviewer decision
- replay chronology
- verification receipt
- audit-ready report

Traditional controls can protect endpoints, communications and credentials. Cyber Sentinels focuses on whether the decision itself has a reviewable trust trail.

## Hiring Security Wedge

Hiring Security is the commercial wedge because the hiring funnel is now a fraud frontline.

Fake candidates can become enterprise access risk when they move through recruiting, interviews, onboarding and privileged workflow access. Proxy interviews, AI-assisted interview fraud and session integrity anomalies are operational risks that security, legal and people teams need to review together.

Cyber Sentinels frames hiring risk as:

- fake applicants entering sensitive-role pipelines
- proxy interview participation
- AI-assisted answers or generated interview presence
- injected audio, video or meeting feeds
- identity confidence that diverges from session integrity
- governance escalation before access or hiring decisions advance

This remains evidence-first and non-surveillance. Flags are review context, not automatic conclusions.

## Proof-Derived Authorization Roadmap

Every autonomous action will need identity, authorization and evidence.

Cyber Sentinels should continue to describe agent governance as roadmap language tied to proof-derived authorization, not as a new runtime-control system in the current product.

Roadmap terms:

- authorization lineage
- governed execution
- signed activity
- evidence chains
- accountable ownership
- human governance before sensitive action

The near-term product narrative should say that agents and autonomous workflows need signed, reviewable activity with clear identity, owner, purpose, authorization scope and evidence. It should not imply that Cyber Sentinels currently enforces all agent runtime actions.

## What Is Implemented Now

The current product already supports the positioning through existing surfaces:

- homepage and enterprise copy for enterprise workflow trust
- Hiring Security page and interview risk dashboard language
- Session Integrity review language
- Governance Review with reviewer ownership and action state
- Trust Replay and replay chronology
- Verification Receipts and protected receipt pages
- evidence chain and audit-ready report framing
- agent identity and autonomy governance roadmap pages

The implemented value is the operational chain:

session integrity -> evidence -> governance -> replay -> receipt

This is enough to explain Cyber Sentinels as decision-layer trust defense without adding tables, APIs or speculative routes.

## What Is Deferred

Deferred work should remain clearly separated from implemented capability:

- no new synthetic trust tables
- no new agent runtime APIs
- no speculative autonomous-action enforcement routes
- no weakened auth, RLS or reviewer gates
- no overclaiming detection accuracy
- no claims of full agent-control infrastructure

Future work may expand proof-derived authorization, agent activity signing, evidence-chain enforcement and governed execution, but only after the product, policy and data boundaries are defined.
