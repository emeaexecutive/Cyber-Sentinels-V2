# Deferred Scope

Date: 2026-06-11

This registry protects Cyber Sentinels from product sprawl during enterprise pilot conversations. The current platform focus is operational trust, explainable governance, evidence chains, verification receipts and replayable timelines.

## Explicitly Deferred

- Advanced biometrics.
- Autonomous trust systems.
- Full C2PA integrations.
- Cryptographic attestation systems.
- Advanced ML scoring.
- Large-scale orchestration infrastructure.

## Pilot Boundary

Deferred items may be discussed as future roadmap only. They should not be sold as active production capability, used as launch-critical dependencies, or presented as automated truth infrastructure.

## Current Operational Focus

- Hiring Security.
- Explainable Governance.
- Verification Receipts.
- Replayable Trust Timelines.
- Evidence Chains.
- Human-Governed Review.

## Positioning Guardrails

- Detection and provenance are signals, not certainty claims.
- AI may assist with summaries and recommendations, but humans review and decide.
- Missing optional integrations should create warnings, not false outages.
- Trust scores are explainable operational indicators, not autonomous judgments.
