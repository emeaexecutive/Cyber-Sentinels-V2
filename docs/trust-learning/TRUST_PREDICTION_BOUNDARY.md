# Trust Prediction boundary

Trust Prediction™ in Epic 34 means a confidence-bounded historical comparison: recurrence, trend continuation, likely additional-evidence need, or likely review need among comparable prior cases. It never predicts future misconduct, malicious intent, fraud or a future incident.

Every output includes the named comparison population, sample size, matching-case count, time window, evidence coverage, confidence classification, limitations, `noCausalClaim: true`, and `futureMisconductPrediction: false`. Example: “Comparable prior cases matched this evidence-backed pattern in 8 of 10 reviewed instances.”

The output is derived guidance for a reviewer. It is not executable, not a canonical decision and not a verified future fact.
