# Federated Workflow Trust Foundations

Cyber Sentinels provides federated workflow trust infrastructure for enterprises, identities and autonomous systems.

This foundation is intentionally lightweight. It links workflow evidence, governance continuity, replayable trust history and authorization lineage without adding massive graph infrastructure, surveillance systems or hidden identity tracking.

## Trust Network Philosophy

Federated workflow trust means enterprise trust becomes more useful when workflow records remain connected over time:

- workflow to evidence
- evidence to replay
- replay to governance outcome
- governance outcome to authorization event
- authorization event to trust posture
- trust posture to workflow outcome

The goal is not a universal identity score. The goal is a reviewable network of enterprise workflow evidence that explains how trust changed and who governed the change.

## Governance And Evidence Principles

Cyber Sentinels should keep governance and evidence at the center:

- reviewer attribution remains visible
- escalation ownership remains attached to the workflow
- provider evidence stays explainable
- workflow outcomes remain replayable
- authorization changes retain lineage
- evidence chains remain tied to the state they influenced

Trust state should change because a governed workflow record changed, not because of hidden monitoring.

## Replayable Workflow Trust

Replay is the operational memory of the trust network.

Replay should connect:

- verification events
- provider-backed evidence
- shared anomaly indicators
- governance review
- reviewer interventions
- authorization changes
- workflow outcomes
- receipts and audit-ready reports

Replayable workflow trust lets enterprises inspect a decision without claiming perfect detection, biometric certainty or automated authenticity judgment.

## Authorization Lineage

Authorization lineage explains how permission changed:

- who or what requested access
- what evidence supported the request
- which provider or workflow signal contributed context
- which reviewer owned the escalation
- what governance outcome was recorded
- whether a receipt or replay chronology exists

For autonomous systems, authorization lineage should include declared purpose, permission scope, governed execution, signed activity and revocation history.

## Federated Signal Concepts

Federated trust signals are shared enterprise indicators derived from governed workflow evidence.

Examples:

- repeated session-integrity anomalies
- missing provider evidence patterns
- recurring governance escalation reasons
- authorization changes after evidence review
- workflow outcomes linked to replay history
- provider-backed signals attached to receipts

These signals should support governance intelligence. They should not become centralized surveillance, social scoring or hidden identity tracking.

## Ethical Boundaries

Cyber Sentinels should preserve these boundaries:

- no surveillance systems
- no hidden identity tracking
- no universal social scoring
- no speculative biometric claims
- no automatic truth claims
- no centralized behavioral profiling
- no weakening auth or RLS to increase visibility

Federated workflow trust is consent-based, enterprise-governed and evidence-first.

## Future Direction

Future trust graph work should remain incremental:

- connect existing workflow relationships more clearly
- expose authorization lineage on replay and receipts
- show evidence continuity across workflow outcomes
- summarize federated anomaly indicators for reviewers
- keep provider evidence explainable
- preserve governance ownership as the authority layer

Do not build massive graph infrastructure until real workflow demand justifies it.
