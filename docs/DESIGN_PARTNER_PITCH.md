# Cyber Sentinels Design Partner Pitch

## The engagement

Cyber Sentinels works with a small number of enterprise design partners to evaluate operational trust in one consequential workflow. The pilot is structured to test concrete evidence, governance and continuity requirements, not to promise certainty or replace existing identity, security or compliance systems.

We verify the actor, the workflow and the evidence behind critical operations.

## What the enterprise receives

- A mapped trust journey for one selected workflow.
- An inventory of human identities, AI agents, service accounts and API actors in scope.
- A review of authorization lineage, delegated execution and accountable ownership.
- A Trust Posture model grounded in available identity, session, workflow and provider evidence.
- A governance path with review triggers, named ownership and recorded actions.
- Replay continuity showing chronology, posture changes, evidence and operational outcome.
- A findings readout covering validated capabilities, evidence gaps, integration constraints and next-step options.

## Pilot structure

1. **Scope and safeguards** — Select one workflow, stakeholders, data boundaries, success measures and explicit exclusions.
2. **Workflow evaluation** — Map actors, systems, decisions, transitions, evidence sources and operational outcomes.
3. **Governance evaluation** — Define review ownership, escalation triggers, intervention options and retained decision context.
4. **Trust Posture evaluation** — Assess which signals can support a current, explainable posture and where evidence remains incomplete.
5. **Replay continuity evaluation** — Determine whether authorization history, evidence, posture changes and governance actions can be reconstructed coherently.
6. **Operational readout** — Review findings with operations, security, identity, compliance and business owners.

## Candidate workflows

Strong candidates include claims processing, underwriting, customer or workforce onboarding, sensitive workflow approvals, hiring integrity, and AI-agent or service-account execution in regulated processes.

The best pilot has a clear operational owner, meaningful consequence, identifiable evidence sources and a real governance decision path.

## Evaluation questions

- Can the enterprise identify who or what acted and who owns that actor?
- Can delegated authority be traced from grant through change or revocation?
- Can runtime posture changes be explained from retained evidence?
- Can governance intervention be assigned and reconstructed?
- Can replay preserve workflow verification continuity after execution ends?
- Can the result support practical review without presenting inference as certainty?

## Boundaries

The pilot does not certify that an identity, agent or workflow is universally trustworthy. It does not replace human governance, create autonomous punitive decisions or claim integrations that have not been validated. Findings reflect the evidence and systems available during the agreed evaluation window.

## A useful outcome

A successful design partnership gives the enterprise a defensible view of how operational trust can be maintained across one real workflow, where current controls provide sufficient evidence, where continuity breaks, and whether Cyber Sentinels can close those gaps without creating another fragmented control layer.
