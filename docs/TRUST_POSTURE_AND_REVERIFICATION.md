# Trust Posture and Reverification

## Trust Freshness Philosophy

Cyber Sentinels treats trust as an operational posture that evolves over time.
Verification is not a permanent claim, and confidence should remain explainable
as evidence, workflow context and governance history age.

Core positioning:

> Operational trust posture evolves over time and requires explainable governance.

Cyber Sentinels should support trust freshness without surveillance framing.
Freshness is based on reviewable operational records such as evidence, timeline
events, governance actions, verification receipts and replay sessions. It is not
continuous monitoring, persistent biometric surveillance or aggressive liveness
enforcement.

## Reverification Principles

Reverification is a workflow checkpoint, not a background watcher.

Supported concepts:

- Reverification due states
- Workflow review checkpoints
- Governance-triggered revalidation
- Explainable trust freshness indicators
- Operational confidence aging
- Workflow continuity checks

Reverification should explain:

- What signals or records contributed
- When verification or governance review last occurred
- Whether evidence exists
- Whether governance review is open or complete
- Whether reverification is recommended

Reverification should avoid:

- Invasive continuous monitoring
- Persistent biometric surveillance
- Always-on user tracking
- Automatic trust claims
- Opaque scoring without a clear operational explanation

## Governance Posture Concepts

Governance is the control point for confidence aging. When trust posture becomes
stale, uncertain or unresolved, Cyber Sentinels should route the workflow toward
human review rather than treating the state as final.

Governance posture indicators may include:

- Fresh: recent evidence or review context exists.
- Review checkpoint: confidence is aging and should be reviewed.
- Reverification due: evidence or review context is old or missing.
- Governance review: unresolved action exists and should be resolved first.

These states are operational prompts. They do not automate approvals, denials or
access decisions.

## Operational Trust Continuity

Trust continuity is the ability to explain how a workflow moved from evidence to
review to receipt to replay.

Continuity checks should look for:

- Evidence records linked to the workflow
- Verification receipts that summarize context
- Governance actions and review history
- Timeline events showing operational sequence
- Replay sessions preserving review memory
- Signals that contributed context without becoming final judgment

Operational confidence should age when review context becomes old, incomplete or
unresolved. This aging should be visible in workforce posture, governance queues,
verification receipts and replay timelines.

## Integration Points

Reverification and posture indicators should integrate into existing surfaces:

- Workforce Trust: show posture visibility, confidence aging and continuity indicators.
- Governance: show review checkpoints and revalidation prompts on open actions.
- Replay: preserve trust freshness as part of historical reconstruction.
- Verification Receipts: explain receipt freshness and whether reverification is recommended.
- Evidence Chains: keep freshness tied to reviewable records, not hidden monitoring.

## Deferred Federation Concepts

The following concepts are documented only and must not be implemented in the
current platform phase:

- Consortium trust networks
- Cross-tenant intelligence sharing
- Shared behavioral graphs
- Federated trust scoring
- Multi-organization trust reputation exchange

These remain future exploratory concepts. Current Cyber Sentinels posture work
must stay tenant-local, evidence-backed, human-governed and explainable.

## Public Positioning

Use:

- Operational Trust Infrastructure with explainable trust posture workflows.
- Trust freshness.
- Reverification checkpoints.
- Governance review intervals.
- Workflow continuity checks.
- Explainable confidence states.

Avoid:

- Surveillance framing
- Always monitoring language
- Autonomous trust claims
- Persistent biometric monitoring
- Cross-tenant intelligence promises
