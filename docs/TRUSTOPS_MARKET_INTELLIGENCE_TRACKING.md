# TrustOps Market Intelligence Tracking

## Purpose

Track market signals that affect Cyber Sentinels category timing, enterprise priorities, provider policy and workflow expansion. Record source, publication date, event date, affected market, evidence quality and strategic implication. Do not convert market headlines into unsupported product claims.

## Tracking categories

### AI-agent security

- agent identity and ownership;
- tool-use and delegated-action controls;
- runtime policy enforcement;
- agent incident and accountability patterns; and
- agent observability versus governed execution.

### Non-human identity governance

- service-account and API-actor growth;
- NHI inventory and ownership;
- credential and authorization lineage;
- delegated authority and revocation; and
- convergence of NHI and AI-agent governance.

### Enterprise AI sovereignty

- customer data ownership requirements;
- regional processing and residency;
- private, sovereign and enterprise AI deployments;
- prompt, workflow and operational-memory ownership; and
- procurement requirements for provider flexibility.

### AI-provider risk

- training and retention policy changes;
- enterprise contractual controls;
- provider outages and concentration risk;
- model deprecation and migration risk;
- data leakage incidents; and
- regulatory or legal actions affecting provider use.

### Workflow verification

- verification inside business processes;
- evidence continuity across systems;
- approval and exception integrity;
- verification receipts and auditability; and
- regulated workflow assurance requirements.

### Runtime trust

- session and execution integrity;
- trust-state changes during workflows;
- continuous verification and reverification;
- authorization drift; and
- governance intervention during execution.

### Deepfake and interview fraud

- proxy interviews and synthetic applicants;
- injected camera, audio or screen sessions;
- identity evidence reuse;
- enterprise loss or response data; and
- hiring-platform and provider countermeasures.

### Operational trust infrastructure

- TrustOps category language;
- identity, security, governance and operations convergence;
- replay, chronology and operational-memory products;
- control-plane and orchestration positioning; and
- evidence-backed human accountability.

### Acquisitions and funding

- acquisitions across identity, NHI, agent security and AI governance;
- funding rounds and valuation signals;
- enterprise buyer and strategic investor activity;
- category consolidation; and
- capability gaps implied by transactions.

### Frontier-lab enterprise tension

- customer concerns about provider dependency;
- competition between model capability and enterprise control;
- data ownership and training disputes;
- enterprise migration or multi-provider strategies; and
- opportunities for a provider-agnostic TrustOps layer.

## Review cadence

- Weekly: notable product, policy, funding and incident signals.
- Monthly: category synthesis, competitor movement and enterprise implications.
- Quarterly: market thesis updates, workflow priorities and provider-governance changes.

## Evidence discipline

For every tracked item capture:

- source and direct link;
- publication date and event date;
- primary versus secondary source;
- confirmed fact versus inference;
- relevance to current product capability;
- whether action is required; and
- owner and next review date.

Market intelligence informs strategy. It does not establish compliance, prove customer demand or authorize optimistic integration claims.
