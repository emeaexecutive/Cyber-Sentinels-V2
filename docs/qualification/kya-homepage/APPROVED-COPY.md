## Section heading

Know Your AI Agent

## Main headline

Knowing who an AI agent is doesn't mean knowing what it's authorised to do.

## Supporting copy

AI agents are increasingly acting on behalf of people and organisations — accessing business systems, interacting with services and initiating consequential transactions.

But identity alone is not enough.

Cyber Sentinels is developing KYA to help organisations understand which AI agent is acting, who is responsible for it, what authority it has been given and whether a requested action is permitted under current policy.

Through TENENTE™, our vision extends beyond agent identity towards accountable, policy-governed execution.

## Five capabilities

- IDENTIFY — Know which AI agent is requesting an action.
- VERIFY — Establish ownership and delegated authority.
- AUTHORISE — Determine whether the requested action is permitted.
- ENFORCE — Apply ALLOW, REVIEW or DENY before controlled execution.
- PROVE — Preserve evidence of the decision and outcome.
