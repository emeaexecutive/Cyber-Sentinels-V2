# Design Partner Outreach

## Target buyers

- CISOs and security leaders responsible for identity risk, insider risk, and fraud prevention.
- Heads of Talent Acquisition handling high-volume or high-trust hiring.
- HR operations leaders responsible for interview process integrity.
- Risk, fraud, and compliance leaders who need evidence for sensitive workflow decisions.
- Founders, COOs, and people leaders at AI-exposed companies hiring remotely.
- Staffing, recruiting, and RPO leaders whose customers are asking for stronger candidate assurance.

## Pain points

- Fake applicants are reaching interview and offer stages.
- Proxy interviews and AI-assisted answers are difficult to prove after the fact.
- Video, voice, device, and session anomalies are scattered across tools.
- Teams lack a calm way to escalate suspicious sessions into Governance Review.
- Existing systems generate alerts, but not always a clear chronology or receipt.
- Rejecting or escalating a candidate without evidence creates legal, compliance, and reputational risk.

## Pilot ask

Run a focused two-to-four week design partner pilot around one high-risk hiring workflow.

The ask:

- Share current hiring integrity pain points and review workflow.
- Walk through three to five recent or representative risk scenarios.
- Use Cyber Sentinels demo-safe flows to validate the trust journey, replay, and receipt model.
- Join one kickoff, one midpoint review, and one closeout call.
- Provide feedback on evidence language, reviewer actions, receipt format, and pilot readiness.

No production dependency is required for the first conversation. The goal is to validate whether the evidence model fits the buyer's real workflow.

## Pilot value

- A clear view of how synthetic identity risk could be represented in the hiring process.
- A shared language across talent, security, compliance, and leadership.
- Demo-safe replay and receipt artifacts that show how decisions would be explained.
- A practical pilot plan without asking the buyer to replace their ATS, identity provider, or security stack.
- Early influence over the product's hiring security workflow, evidence labels, and governance model.

## Call script

### Opening

Thanks for taking the time. I am looking for design partner feedback, not asking you to change systems today. The question I am trying to validate is simple: when a hiring workflow looks suspicious, what evidence does your team need to make and explain a decision?

### Discovery

- Where are you seeing the most hiring integrity concern today?
- Are fake applicants, proxy interviews, AI-assisted answers, or video and voice mismatch showing up in your process?
- Who owns the decision when something looks suspicious: talent, security, legal, compliance, or the hiring manager?
- What evidence do you currently have after the session ends?
- What is hard to prove or explain later?

### Product framing

Cyber Sentinels does not try to replace your ATS, identity provider, or security stack. It creates an operational trust layer around the workflow. The product connects verification evidence, Session Integrity, Governance Review, Replay Evidence, and Verification Receipts so teams can understand what happened and record a human decision.

### Demo transition

The quickest way to explain it is through the hiring attack demo: a fake candidate enters the workflow, verification begins, session integrity fails, Governance Review opens, Replay Evidence appears, reviewer action is recorded, and a Verification Receipt is issued.

### Pilot close

If this maps to your pain, the next step would be a lightweight design partner pilot: one workflow, a few representative scenarios, and structured feedback on the evidence model. The outcome is a clear pilot plan and a shared view of whether Cyber Sentinels can help your hiring, security, and compliance teams work from the same record.
