# Design Partner Strategy

## Purpose

Cyber Sentinels is onboarding a limited number of early collaborators and operational design partners to validate whether governed trust workflows are understandable, credible and useful in real enterprise contexts.

Cyber Sentinels is evolving through operational collaboration and real-world workflow feedback.

## Target Partner Profiles

- Enterprise operations teams managing approvals, exceptions or sensitive review paths.
- Governance, risk and compliance teams exploring AI oversight and auditability.
- Trust and safety teams that need evidence-backed decision records.
- Security or identity teams working with permissions, provenance or agent access.
- Workflow owners piloting AI-assisted internal operations.
- Regulated or high-accountability teams where human escalation remains required.

## Operational Use Cases

- AI-assisted workflow approvals with human accountability.
- Human review escalation for incomplete, sensitive or high-risk cases.
- Operational audit trails across evidence, decisions and review notes.
- Evidence-backed verification for people, organizations, workflows or agents.
- Trust event visibility for changes in state, permissions or verification status.
- Governance workflows that map policy into repeatable review steps.

## Strongest Market Signals

- Teams already piloting AI-assisted workflows but lacking clear review records.
- Buyers asking how AI recommendations can be audited after the fact.
- Concern about unclear ownership when automated systems influence decisions.
- Need for operational evidence trails rather than abstract trust scores.
- Interest in trust events, provenance and accountable workflow visibility.

## Onboarding Observations

- Start with one real workflow rather than a broad platform tour.
- Ask where decisions are currently delayed, disputed or hard to audit.
- Capture the current review path, evidence sources and escalation owners.
- Identify what would make a trust event operationally useful to the team.
- Keep language concrete: evidence, review, approval, escalation, audit trail.

## Emerging Wedge Patterns

- AI governance readiness for teams moving from pilots into operations.
- Evidence-backed approval trails for sensitive internal workflows.
- Human escalation layers for AI-assisted decisions.
- Trust event visibility for governance and operational leadership.
- Provenance and accountability infrastructure for agentic workflows.
