# Enterprise Workflow Trust Positioning

## Position

Organizations can no longer reliably verify the people, sessions and workflows entering critical systems.

Cyber Sentinels provides operational trust infrastructure for hiring, verification and enterprise workflow governance.

## Narrative Focus

Cyber Sentinels should be presented as a workflow trust layer, not a detector-only platform or experimental AI tool. The product connects:

- Evidence
- Replay
- Governance
- Workflow Trust
- Session Integrity
- Verification Receipts

The enterprise story is practical: preserve what happened, assign review ownership, replay the chronology, record the outcome and issue an audit-grade receipt.

## Workflow Trust Model

1. A person, session or workflow enters a critical system.
2. Evidence is recorded and kept reviewable.
3. Session integrity and authorization context are checked as separate states.
4. Governance review opens when workflow risk needs named ownership.
5. Replay reconstructs the chronology without rewriting it.
6. A verification receipt records the evidence, reviewer action, governance outcome and workflow link.

## Demo Story

The clearest demo path is:

- fake candidate enters the hiring workflow
- session integrity anomaly appears
- governance escalation opens
- replay evidence is generated
- reviewer action records the workflow outcome
- verification receipt is issued

This keeps the demo operational, accountable and understandable without over-indexing on detection.

## Receipt Standard

Verification receipts should feel:

- portable
- enterprise-safe
- audit-grade
- workflow-linked

They should explain what was reviewed, who acted, what evidence remains, which replay reference exists and whether any governance action is still pending.

## Guardrails

Do not add speculative systems, new infrastructure or new database tables for positioning work.

Do not claim perfect detection. Detection can support a review, but evidence, replay, governance and workflow accountability determine the enterprise trust story.
