# Investor Mode

## Epic 36 Question

Why does this become infrastructure rather than another AI security product?

## Answer

The answer comes from the working platform. Customer-owned operational history, Trust Memory, Replay, Authority Lineage, Evidence Graph, Enterprise Decision History, reviewer corrections and historical outcomes create cumulative value over time.

## Moat framing

The platform remains valuable even if foundation models become free because the moat comes from accumulated enterprise knowledge and replayable operational history rather than model access alone.
