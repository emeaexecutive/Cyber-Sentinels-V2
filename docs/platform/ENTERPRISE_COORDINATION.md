# Enterprise Coordination

## Epic 36 Purpose

The coordination layer ties enterprise trust views together around the canonical platform services. The goal is to keep the experience explainable and grounded in evidence rather than create another dashboard for Epic 36.

## Coordination flow

1. People and AI agents are surfaced together with their authority context.
2. Evidence and replay chronology are linked to the operational decision.
3. Trust Memory and Learning provide persistent context for future recommendations.
4. Executive and investor summaries remain grounded in the same record.

## Outcome

This design keeps the platform useful for security, compliance, operations and executive review while avoiding duplicate implementation paths.
