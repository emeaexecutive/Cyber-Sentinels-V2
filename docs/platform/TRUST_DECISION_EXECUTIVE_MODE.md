# Trust Decision Executive Mode

## Audiences

Epic 38 Executive Mode produces views for Board, CEO, CISO, Audit, Legal, Risk, Operations, and Finance. All views are projections of the same canonical Trust Decision; they do not fork facts or create audience-specific outcomes.

## Required report

Every report contains:

- what happened;
- why it happened;
- business impact;
- operational impact;
- remaining uncertainty;
- recommended next actions;
- Decision Health; and
- the canonical Replay reference.

What happened, why, both impacts, and recommended actions are citation-bearing statements. Remaining uncertainty comes directly from unresolved known unknowns. Recommended actions fail validation if they cite evidence outside the decision.

## Design Partner Mode

The design-partner projection demonstrates Candidate → Identity → Authority → Evidence → Decision → Replay → Trust Memory → Trust Journey → Recovery → Executive Summary. Each stage carries a canonical reference. Recovery is shown as `NOT_RECORDED` when it did not occur.

## Investor Mode

Investor Mode reports retained decisions, replay linkage, reviewer enrichment, recovery, unique evidence, and evolution-event counts. These are counts of preserved records, not invented ROI. The compounding asset is the enterprise's attributable decision history and reviewer/outcome context.

## Future model readiness

Provider-neutral interfaces exist for Decision Similarity, Evidence Ranking, Policy Recommendation, Recovery Recommendation, and Reviewer Assistance. Requests are limited to explicitly permitted evidence IDs. Responses must cite that evidence and declare that they are non-authoritative and cannot mutate trust, policy, authority, or evidence. Epic 38 trains and invokes no specialist model.
