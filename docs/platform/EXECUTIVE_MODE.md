# Executive Mode

## Epic 36 Audience

Executive mode is designed for the CEO, Board, CISO, Risk Committee, Audit, Compliance, Legal, Operations and Finance stakeholders.

## Required summary fields

Every summary identifies:

- Known facts
- Evidence
- Unknowns
- Open risks
- Recommended actions
- Confidence

## Guidance

The executive summary should remain bounded by what the platform can actually support. Missing evidence is called out as missing rather than silently inferred.
