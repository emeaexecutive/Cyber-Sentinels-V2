# Enterprise Trust Platform™

## Epic 36 scope

Cyber Sentinels evolves from an operational trust intelligence platform into the Enterprise Trust Platform™. The platform coordinates enterprise trust across people, AI agents, identities, credentials, devices, applications, providers, policies, evidence, runtime, authority, business outcomes, economics, incidents and recovery.

## Architectural rule

Every included capability consumes an existing canonical system instead of creating a duplicate trust engine. The RC1 coordination experience reuses Trust Fabric, Replay, Trust Memory, Evidence Graph, Authority Lineage, Enterprise Decision History, Operational Trust, Trust Journey projections and Trust Decision Intelligence. Trust Learning, Trust Simulation and Trust Resilience remain excluded independent or future work.

## Coordination surface

The Enterprise Trust Platform experience provides a coordinated view that answers:

- What happened?
- Why?
- Who was responsible?
- Which authority existed?
- What evidence supports this?
- What business process was affected?
- What AI agents and providers participated?
- What decisions changed?
- What is the recommended action?
- Can this be replayed and independently verified?

## Execution boundary

AI assistance remains provider-neutral and non-authoritative. It can summarize evidence or note uncertainty, but it cannot change trust, authority, evidence, policy or approval outcomes.
