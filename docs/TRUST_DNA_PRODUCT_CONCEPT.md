# Trust DNA™ Product Concept

**Trust DNA™ shows how operational trust has evolved within a defined enterprise context.**

Trust DNA is the visual representation of a Living Trust Profile. It uses calm assurance cards, contextual labels, evidence coverage, authority lineage and an explained timeline. It deliberately avoids a helix, gamified score, red/green judgment of a person or one permanent number.

The eight dimensions are Identity assurance, Authority assurance, Credential assurance, Runtime integrity, Evidence quality, Behavioural consistency, Governance status and Decision confidence. Every dimension exposes its current state, reason, source evidence, last change, recorded expiry, limitation and reviewer status.

Trust DNA is not biological information, a global identity or employment score, a fraud label, a reputation product or an autonomous decision-maker. Behavioural observations are contextual evidence only. Human governance remains authoritative for approval, pause, revocation, restoration and override.
