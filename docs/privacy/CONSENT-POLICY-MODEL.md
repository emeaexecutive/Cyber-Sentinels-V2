# Consent Policy Model

The initial profiles are EEA, UK, US_GENERAL, US_OPT_OUT and GLOBAL_DEFAULT. Region selection uses a configured profile or a coarse two-letter country header. Unknown location receives GLOBAL_DEFAULT, the strict profile. Full IP addresses are neither read nor stored by the consent domain.

Every profile starts with optional technology denied until a known, integrity-protected choice exists. EEA, UK and GLOBAL_DEFAULT are opt-in. US_OPT_OUT records an opt-out mode and after-notice capability for deployments that approve it, but does not pre-enable trackers before the notice. This is configuration behavior, not a legal conclusion.

Essential is always active. Functional, Analytics, AI Improvements and Marketing are optional. Policy version changes invalidate old cookies and cause the banner to return. Policy activation, re-consent requirements, locale and effective dates must pass enterprise review. Nothing here claims certification or universal compliance.
