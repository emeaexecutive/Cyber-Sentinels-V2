# Cyber Sentinels Voiceover And Caption Pack

## Voice Direction

- Calm, assured and human; international enterprise tone
- Approximately 125 words per minute
- Documentary observation, not trailer intensity
- Pause 300ms after the opening and 500ms before the close
- Pronounce Cyber Sentinels as `SY-ber SEN-tuh-nuhls`

## 90-Second Voiceover

AI changed trust.

What looks real can now be generated, substituted or injected into the systems where critical decisions begin.

In enterprise hiring, a credible profile and a convincing interview feed are no longer enough. Synthetic identities and injected sessions can move through workflows built for a different era.

Cyber Sentinels keeps identity and session integrity connected, but distinct. Liveness, media risk, injection risk and channel evidence remain explainable signals, never automatic verdicts.

When the session changes, the workflow responds. Evidence is preserved. Policy is applied. An accountable reviewer receives the escalation and decides what happens next.

Every material transition becomes part of an audit trail. Replay reconstructs the evidence, session changes and governance actions in order, without rewriting history.

A verification receipt records what was checked, what changed, who acted and why, giving security, talent and compliance teams one audit-ready explanation.

This is operational trust: evidence, governance and memory for AI-era workflows.

The future of AI is not only intelligence. It is trust.
