## WORKING BUT NEEDS LIVE PROVIDER/STAGING PROOF

- Identity correlation is implemented for configured providers, but each production provider/configuration still needs retained live qualification evidence.
- Synthetic-media/deepfake handling consumes provider evidence; Cyber Sentinels does not natively detect deepfakes, liveness, biometrics or document authenticity.
- Grounded AI governance has a real server-side structured-output model path, citation allowlist/validator and deterministic fallback; credentialed staging execution and retained output/audit proof remain outstanding.
- Trust Recovery is real when reconstructed from persisted canonical history; the separate public workflow remains an explicitly labelled simulation.
- Blast Radius and Trust Cascade algorithms exist, but Trust Centre needs complete persisted tenant graph data before showing calculated impact.
- Provider replacement continuity is staging-proven by retained preview evidence, not live-provider-proven.
- External execution keeps request, acknowledgement and outcome separate; an actual relay/destination observation is still required for live outcome proof.

## NOT YET REAL

- Native Cyber Sentinels deepfake, biometric, liveness and document-authenticity detection: not implemented and not publicly claimed as native.
- Autonomous AI trust decisions or AI enforcement: not implemented and explicitly prohibited; AI is advisory and cannot override the canonical decision engine.
- Independently confirmed live outcomes without an independent destination observation: not implemented and represented as `UNKNOWN` or provider-asserted only.
- Live-provider proof for this branch: absent; no registry entry is marked `LIVE_PROVIDER_PROVEN`.
