# TENENTE™ canonical product definition

Status: canonical source for TENENTE positioning. Public copy, metadata, GTM
agent instructions, developer, enterprise and investor material must align with
this file. Changes require founder approval.

## Canonical statement (preserve exactly)

TENENTE is the product direction within Cyber Sentinels: helping organisations apply current policy and delegated authority to AI agent actions, then preserve evidence of the decision.

## Identity

- **Cyber Sentinels** is the overarching enterprise AI trust and execution-control platform.
- **TENENTE™** is the integrated product direction within it, focused on agent authority, delegation, execution-time decisions and accountable autonomous operations.

TENENTE is **not**:

- a separate company or unrelated product;
- a separate security platform;
- a replacement for Cyber Sentinels;
- merely a marketing campaign;
- an independent repository, database, Vercel project, identity provider or commercial billing system.

## Canonical architecture

Identity → Authority → Policy → Runtime Trust Signals → Decision → Execution → Receipt → Replay → Trust Memory™

TENENTE reuses the existing Cyber Sentinels registries, decision engine, evidence stores, tenant model and authentication. It must not duplicate them.

## Authority-control responsibilities

- Agent identity and registration.
- Delegated authority and scope.
- Policy evaluation at execution time.
- ALLOW / REVIEW / DENY decisions.
- Authority expiry and revocation.
- Swarm and multi-agent delegation.
- Runtime enforcement boundaries.
- Signed cancellation and containment.
- Evidence receipts.
- Replay and Trust Memory™.

## Approved lines (preserve exactly)

- Built in Aragón. Designed for a world of autonomous agents.
- Authority can be delegated. It cannot be invented.

## Claim precision

Label each capability as a concept, a locally tested capability, or a hosted/Production-qualified capability, and only use the last label where independent evidence exists. Local tests do not establish hosted or Production readiness. TENENTE Swarm cancellation and emergency containment are not Production-qualified.

## Development isolation

Swarm and GTM worktrees may stay separate for engineering safety, but they share one Cyber Sentinels repository and one product architecture. Do not create a separate TENENTE Supabase project, Vercel project, identity provider or billing system.
