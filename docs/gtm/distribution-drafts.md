## LinkedIn draft 1 — identity is not authority

An AI agent can have a verified identity and still lack permission for a
particular action.

Identity answers who or what is acting. Authority answers what that actor may
do, for which purpose and under which current policy.

That distinction matters when an agent can use tools or work across systems.
A previous approval is not permanent authority when the action, target or
policy changes.

Cyber Sentinels is focused on helping organisations determine whether agents
are authorised to act and preserve evidence of the decision.

## LinkedIn draft 2 — ALLOW · REVIEW · DENY

ALLOW · REVIEW · DENY are only useful when the outcome has a clear meaning at
the boundary that controls an action.

REVIEW should hold a request for accountable review. DENY should stop the
controlled request. An ALLOW decision alone is not proof that a downstream
system completed an action.

Good agent governance connects authority, current policy and evidence—and
states where local testing ends and hosted qualification begins.

**Founder review required. Do not post without approval.**
