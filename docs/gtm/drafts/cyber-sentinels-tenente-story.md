# Cyber Sentinels and TENENTE™: The Story Behind the Mission

**Question:** What connects Cyber Sentinels, TENENTE™ and the idea of
authority for autonomous agents?

**Built in Aragón. Designed for a world of autonomous agents.**

TENENTE™ draws inspiration from the historical concept of watchfulness and
guardianship. The founder's connection to Aragón, Spain, gives the story a
place and a perspective: responsibility is not abstract when people and
organisations entrust important work to systems that can act.

Cyber Sentinels is building around a practical question: how can an
organisation determine whether an AI agent is authorised to act under current
policy, and preserve evidence of the decision? TENENTE is the product
direction within Cyber Sentinels for bringing that question into operational
workflows.

The principle is simple: **Authority can be delegated. It cannot be
invented.** Permission must come from an accountable organisation, remain
bounded by that organisation's policy and be reviewable when circumstances
change. An agent's identity or a previous approval is not a substitute for
current authority.

This story is a mission statement, not a claim that every capability is
available in Production. Some API and Swarm paths have local tests; hosted
qualification is separate. TENENTE Swarm cancellation and emergency
containment are not Production-qualified.

Read the Cyber Sentinels [About page](/about) and the customer-facing
[platform overview](/platform). Both explain the public story without
disclosing internal implementation details.
