# Cyber Sentinels Cinematic Launch Script

## Production Brief

- Runtime: 90 seconds
- Audience: enterprise security, talent, governance and compliance leaders
- Format: 16:9 master with center-safe framing for 9:16 and 1:1
- Tone: cinematic realism, calm authority and operational credibility
- Guardrail: risk signals inform review; accountable humans make decisions

## 90-Second Master

### 0:00-0:08 | The Trust Shift

**Picture:** Black resolves into a credible remote interview in a quiet enterprise office. Slow push toward the screen.

**Voiceover:** "AI changed trust. What looks real can now be generated, substituted or injected into the systems where critical decisions begin."

**Super:** `AI CHANGED TRUST.`

### 0:08-0:20 | Synthetic Hiring Attack

**Picture:** A hiring manager reviews a polished profile. The interview remains visually natural while a restrained channel overlay reveals that the media source has changed.

**Voiceover:** "In enterprise hiring, a credible profile and a convincing interview feed are no longer enough. Synthetic identities and injected sessions can move through workflows built for a different era."

**Super:** `CREDIBLE APPEARANCE IS NOT VERIFIABLE PROVENANCE.`

### 0:20-0:34 | Session Integrity

**Picture:** Identity stays linked while liveness, media risk, injection risk and channel evidence appear as distinct states. Injection risk moves calmly from neutral to elevated.

**Voiceover:** "Cyber Sentinels keeps identity and session integrity connected, but distinct. Liveness, media risk, injection risk and channel evidence remain explainable signals, never automatic verdicts."

**Super:** `IDENTITY LINKED / INJECTION RISK ELEVATED / REVIEW REQUIRED`

### 0:34-0:48 | Governance Escalation

**Picture:** The workflow pauses. Evidence, policy context and reason codes assemble into a review panel. A named human owner receives the escalation.

**Voiceover:** "When the session changes, the workflow responds. Evidence is preserved. Policy is applied. An accountable reviewer receives the escalation and decides what happens next."

**Super:** `AI ASSISTS. HUMANS DECIDE.`

### 0:48-1:02 | Audit Trail And Replay

**Picture:** A replay timeline builds in order: identity check, session change, elevated signal, reviewer assignment, evidence review and governance action.

**Voiceover:** "Every material transition becomes part of an audit trail. Replay reconstructs the evidence, session changes and governance actions in order, without rewriting history."

**Super:** `READ-ONLY OPERATIONAL REPLAY`

### 1:02-1:16 | Verification Receipt

**Picture:** The timeline resolves into a receipt showing identity state, session integrity, evidence references, reviewer context, governance outcome and timestamp.

**Voiceover:** "A verification receipt records what was checked, what changed, who acted and why, giving security, talent and compliance teams one audit-ready explanation."

**Super:** `EXPLAINABLE VERIFICATION RECEIPT`

### 1:16-1:25 | Operational Trust

**Picture:** Hiring, governance, evidence and receipt surfaces align. Cut to founder.

**Founder:** "This is operational trust: evidence, governance and memory for AI-era workflows."

### 1:25-1:30 | Close

**Picture:** Cyber Sentinels wordmark on black. Hold two seconds after the line.

**Voiceover:** "The future of AI is not only intelligence. It is trust."

**End card:** `Operational Trust Infrastructure for AI-era workflows.`

## Founder Camera Direction

- Calm delivery at 115-125 words per minute; direct eye line and minimal gestures.
- Medium close-up at eye level with a 50-85mm equivalent lens.
- Soft practical key at 45 degrees, warm lamp in depth and cool ambient separation.
- Real enterprise workspace, natural skin texture and documentary style.
- Charcoal or deep-neutral wardrobe without tight patterns or visible logos.
- Deliver the line as a considered conclusion, not a sales slogan.

## Editorial Guardrails

- Use fictional, sample-only data.
- Never imply perfect detection, automated rejection or surveillance.
- Show injection risk as evidence-linked context, not proof of malicious intent.
- Add exact product copy and logos in post-production.
