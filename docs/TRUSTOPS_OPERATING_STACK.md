# TrustOps Operating Stack

## Definition

Cyber Sentinels is the operational trust infrastructure layer for humans, AI agents, non-human identities and enterprise workflows.

TrustOps keeps identity, runtime context, authority, evidence, governance and operational outcomes connected. Persistent Trust Posture explains the current state. Replayable Operational Memory preserves how that state changed.

## The eight layers

### 1. Actor Identity

Records the human, AI agent, service account or API actor entering a workflow, including accountable ownership and available verification context. Identity is a continuing operational input, not a one-time claim of certainty.

### 2. Session Integrity

Preserves runtime, channel, device and continuity signals separately. No individual signal establishes identity or trust by itself; material changes contribute to reviewable posture.

### 3. Authorization Lineage

Connects delegated scope, grants, changes, revocations and policy authority to the actor and workflow. High-impact execution should remain attributable to the authority under which it occurred.

### 4. Evidence Chain

Connects provider evidence, workflow records and integrity observations to source, time and operational context. Evidence remains inspectable and does not become an opaque verdict.

### 5. Governance Review

Routes consequential changes to named reviewers. Escalation rationale, review ownership, intervention and resolution remain part of the operational chronology.

### 6. Replay Timeline

Reconstructs actor, workflow, evidence, authorization, governance, trust change and final outcome. Replay is the canonical operational memory of the workflow, not an auxiliary activity log.

### 7. Persistent Trust Posture

Shows the current explainable state across identity, session, evidence, authorization and governance. Posture can evolve, escalate, decay, recover and require reverification.

### 8. Enterprise AI Sovereignty

Keeps customer operational data, provider use, workflow memory, identity signals and operational IP governed by enterprise policy. Providers contribute bounded evidence; they do not own the trust record.

## Governed execution model

The stack supports one accountable operating path:

1. An actor enters a defined workflow.
2. Session and authorization context establish the operating boundary.
3. Evidence is recorded with its source and time.
4. Trust posture recalculates as context changes.
5. Material changes route to Governance Review.
6. Replay preserves the full chronology.
7. A governed outcome and receipt close the active workflow.

Human review remains authoritative where policy requires escalation. Cyber Sentinels does not claim autonomous truth detection, perfect identity certainty or automatic punitive authority.

## Provider truth

Every provider-facing operational state uses one of four labels:

- **Live** — the supported path is enabled and configured; this is not a claim of uptime or accuracy.
- **Simulated** — controlled demonstration data is in use.
- **Awaiting Credentials** — required server-side configuration is absent.
- **Disabled** — no operational provider result is active.

Configuration and validation caveats remain in provider notes rather than creating optimistic public states.

## Enterprise workflow application

- **Fintech:** preserve actor, API authority, exception evidence and approval history.
- **Insurance:** connect claims intake, automation, adjuster review and governed outcome.
- **Banking:** retain initiator, service-account action, policy checks and dual-control approval.
- **Healthcare:** preserve delegated access, workflow evidence and accountable operational handoffs.
- **Hiring:** connect identity, session integrity, reviewer action and verification receipt.
- **Onboarding:** track human and non-human access as evidence and responsibility change.
- **Claims:** retain source evidence, AI-assisted analysis, reviewer intervention and disposition.
- **Approvals:** preserve who or what acted, under which authority, and why the outcome followed.
- **AI-agent operations:** connect ownership, runtime posture, authorization lineage and replayable execution history.

## Public and protected surfaces

Public pages explain the category, operating model, evidence boundaries and enterprise outcomes. Case records, internal validation, founder controls, launch controls, test benches and raw architecture remain protected, de-indexed and absent from public navigation.

The platform adds no public route for this operating model. Existing homepage, platform, enterprise, Trust Center, Replay, posture, governance and sovereignty surfaces remain the canonical product path.

## Investor and demo readiness

The shortest credible walkthrough follows one governed workflow:

1. identify the human, AI agent or non-human actor and accountable owner;
2. show the authorization and runtime boundary;
3. attach source-linked evidence without claiming certainty;
4. show posture changing as evidence or context changes;
5. route a material change to named governance;
6. replay the complete chronology and governed outcome.

This sequence demonstrates durable infrastructure rather than a collection of features. Demo data and provider-shaped fixtures remain explicitly `Simulated`; only validated, configured operational paths may appear as `Live`.
