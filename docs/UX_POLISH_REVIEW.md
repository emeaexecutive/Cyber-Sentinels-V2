# UX Polish Review

ERM UX work should reduce noise and increase buyer confidence. The priority is operational trust, AI Trust Infrastructure, governance, replay, evidence and sovereignty.

## Findings

| Area | Finding | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public narrative | Strong trust/replay/governance story exists, but route count dilutes it. | Keep public navigation focused on platform, enterprise, trust center, security and design partner conversion. |
| Internal terminology | Many pages expose primitives such as reality OS, origin DNA, trust radar and graph explorer. | Hide or merge speculative primitives into buyer-safe language. |
| Technical leakage | Provider, ML, benchmark and diagnostics routes exist. | Keep protected and describe limitations plainly. |
| Candidate-heavy messaging | Hiring surfaces are useful but can dominate the broader enterprise story. | Keep hiring as a wedge, not the full category. |
| Dashboard duplication | Multiple dashboards represent similar risk/governance concepts. | Merge into dashboard tabs with consistent evidence, replay and next action. |
| Replay | Replay is the strongest trust record concept. | Make replay the canonical operational memory across demos and docs. |
| Sovereignty | Enterprise control of trust records is a differentiator. | Use sovereignty for ownership, policy and evidence control, not vague nationalism. |

## Copy Rules

- Prefer "AI Trust Infrastructure" over broad AI claims.
- Prefer "operational trust", "evidence", "replay", "governance" and "authorization lineage".
- Avoid "certainty", "perfect detection", "real/fake guarantee" and unqualified biometric claims.
- Use "review", "escalate", "evidence incomplete" and "awaiting credentials" where capability is not proven.

## UX Consolidation Backlog

1. Remove public navigation to detection-status, provider-status, benchmark and test-lab surfaces.
2. Merge duplicated trust graph/posture/timeline pages into fewer operator views.
3. Reduce enterprise subpage count by folding evidence into `/enterprise/readiness`.
4. Keep candidate-facing flows protected or workflow-specific.
5. Use replay and evidence receipts as the connective tissue for all demos.
