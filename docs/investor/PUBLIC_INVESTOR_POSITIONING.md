# Public investor positioning

Classification: PUBLIC

## Approved copy

Cyber Sentinels is building infrastructure for a world in which people, AI agents and automated workflows make consequential decisions together.

The opportunity is not another alerting product.

It is the independent operational trust layer connecting identity, authority, evidence, decisions and outcomes.

Design-partner and early-investor conversations are now open.

## Approved category context

Cyber Sentinels is building the Operational Trust Intelligence™ platform for intelligent enterprises.

It transforms fragmented identity, security, AI and operational evidence into continuously explainable, evidence-backed trust decisions.

Most platforms detect, authenticate, monitor or contain. Cyber Sentinels preserves the operational evidence showing what changed, why trust changed, who was accountable and what happened next.

## Claim boundary

Use “building,” “developing,” “designed to,” “design-partner stage,” “pilot stage” where proven, or “available in controlled demonstration” where implementation evidence exists.

Do not publish a fundraising amount, valuation, projected return, unsupported market size or share, unapproved customer name, invented traction, confidential roadmap, implementation detail, security design or technical moat explanation.

Do not claim production deployment, enterprise-scale proof, continuous live monitoring, legal compliance, regulatory approval, autonomous decision-making, guaranteed detection, perfect attribution or predictive certainty.

Any controlled investor material that goes beyond this wording requires the CONTROLLED classification, appropriate access restrictions and a fresh no-blueprint review.
