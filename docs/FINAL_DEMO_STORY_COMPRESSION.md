# Final Demo Story Compression

Date: 2 July 2026

## Outcome

The public and demo narrative now follows one six-step operational story:

1. A person, agent or workflow enters a trusted process.
2. Cyber Sentinels checks identity, Session Integrity and evidence.
3. Trust changes over time.
4. Governance intervenes when risk appears.
5. Replay explains what happened.
6. A Verification Receipt or report preserves the outcome.

This sequence is used across the homepage, demo overview, Demo Lab and Hiring
Security chronology. Trust Center, Trust Evaluation Lab and Replay use the same
terms for the underlying evidence and review states.

## Message compression

The primary positioning is:

- `Operational trust for intelligent systems.`
- `Replayable evidence for trusted workflows.`
- `Trust changes. Cyber Sentinels shows why.`

Repeated hero paragraphs, duplicate access cards, comparison blocks and repeated
demo-proof grids were removed. Existing operational drill-down content remains
available where it explains evidence or workflow state.

Residual abstract platform labels such as trust universe, reality operating
system, synthetic counterpart, trust fabric, trust OS and global trust are not
used in the reviewed surfaces.

## Replay clarity

The controlled Replay Timeline now leads with five plain-language fields:

- event;
- evidence;
- trust change;
- governance action and reviewer; and
- final operational state.

Authorization Lineage remains visible as required operational context. Provider
state, scenario boundary and evidence continuity remain explicit without
presenting an accuracy or authenticity verdict.

## Trust Evaluation Lab clarity

Benchmark cards now show:

- maturity status;
- provider-backed validation state; and
- `Validation required`.

Scenario cards show:

- Concept, Simulated, Prototype or Placeholder maturity;
- provider state;
- provider-backed evidence structure;
- manual-review requirement;
- limitation; and
- `Validation required`.

No benchmark result, provider accuracy, biometric certainty or autonomous
decision is presented.

## CTA standard

Primary public conversion language is limited to:

- `View Demo`
- `Request Enterprise Access`
- `Trust Center`
- `Hiring Security`

Operational links such as Replay Timeline or Verification Receipt remain where
they are needed to inspect a workflow; repeated marketing CTAs were removed.

## Security and system boundaries

No new system, route, database behavior, provider execution path, auth control
or RLS policy was added or changed in this pass.
