# Trust Principles and Category Positioning

## Category thesis

Cyber Sentinels defines the Enterprise Workflow Trust Layer: the operational layer connecting identity verification, provider evidence, session integrity, authorization context, human governance, replay and portable workflow outcomes.

Traditional security protects infrastructure, credentials and communications. Identity providers verify specific claims. Cyber Sentinels addresses the decision continuity between those controls: whether a sensitive workflow remained trustworthy, what changed and why the outcome advanced or stopped.

The category is operational rather than predictive. It is built around reviewable evidence and accountable decisions, not claims of universal intelligence or perfect detection.

## Trust principles

1. **Trust must be explainable.** Operators should understand what changed, why it changed and which evidence informed the outcome.
2. **Trust must be replayable.** Important workflow events, evidence and reviewer actions should remain available in chronological order.
3. **Trust must evolve over time.** Posture should reflect new evidence, session changes, authorization context and governance intervention.
4. **Trust decisions require evidence.** Provider signals and flags are review inputs; sensitive outcomes require traceable records.
5. **Governance must remain human-reviewable.** Named reviewers, escalation reasons and rationale remain visible.
6. **Verification is probabilistic, not absolute.** Verification reduces uncertainty without creating perfect certainty about identity, media or intent.
7. **Operational trust should be auditable.** Outcomes connect to evidence, authorization lineage, replay chronology and a receipt.

## Replay philosophy

Replay is the canonical evidence chronology and workflow reconstruction layer.

It preserves the sequence of:

- workflow entry
- provider verification
- session and signal changes
- evidence additions
- authorization transitions
- escalation and reviewer action
- trust-state changes
- workflow outcome
- receipt issuance

Replay is not a generated story detached from records. It is the read-only operational memory through which an enterprise can reconstruct the evidence and authority behind a decision.

## Explainable trust

Trust posture is specific to a workflow and point in time. Every material transition should show:

- previous state
- current state
- what changed
- why it changed
- contributing evidence
- provider context
- governance impact
- authorization continuity

Scores and confidence labels support review prioritization. They do not represent universal truth or standalone authenticity decisions.

## Governance continuity

Governance keeps four things connected:

1. **Reviewer attribution** — who owns or resolved the review.
2. **Escalation continuity** — why review opened and how it progressed.
3. **Evidence-chain visibility** — what records informed the decision.
4. **Trust-state transition** — how reviewer action affected the workflow outcome.

Governance actions remain visible in replay, verification receipts and evolving trust posture. Human authority remains responsible for sensitive outcomes.

## Enterprise standards

The product should feel like operational infrastructure:

- concise language
- restrained color and animation
- clear type hierarchy
- readable chronology
- explicit ownership
- useful empty states
- evidence before interpretation
- consistent replay and receipt links

Category ownership comes from coherence and operating discipline, not repeated jargon.

## Validation continuity

The protected validation lab demonstrates:

- provider-backed evidence visibility
- failed and pending provider states
- session degradation
- repeated anomalies
- governance intervention
- replay chronology
- authorization changes
- evolving trust posture

Controlled scenarios validate product behavior. They are not accuracy benchmarks.

## Ethical boundaries

Cyber Sentinels does not implement:

- hidden behavioral monitoring
- universal identity scoring
- surveillance across unrelated contexts
- perfect real/fake claims
- speculative AGI authority
- autonomous high-risk decisions without accountable review

Evidence remains purpose-specific, access-controlled and subject to enterprise retention policy. Authentication, admin protection and row-level security remain core boundaries.
