# Competitive Positioning: Runtime Trust and GRC

Static governance platforms commonly organize inventory, obligations and missing documentation. Cyber Sentinels is differentiated by operational questions:

- Who or what is acting?
- Under whose authority?
- Is that authority still valid for this action and context?
- What changed at runtime?
- Why was execution allowed, constrained, paused or blocked?
- Which evidence and receipt support the outcome?
- How did contextual trust change?

This is complementary to GRC. Compliance frameworks are mapped to supporting and missing operational evidence, responsible owner, review date and limitation. Cyber Sentinels does not claim certification and is not a document generator.

`TrustFlow` is not a Cyber Sentinels product name. Public copy must not include unsupported competitor capability claims.
