# Cyber Sentinels Brand and Pilot Consolidation

## Brand positioning

Cyber Sentinels is the primary external brand.

Primary category: Operational Trust Infrastructure.

Core enterprise wedges:

- Hiring Security
- Interview Integrity
- Explainable Governance
- Evidence Chains
- Replayable Trust Timelines

The public story should make Cyber Sentinels easy to understand quickly:

- What it is: enterprise-facing operational trust infrastructure.
- Why it matters: AI-era workflows need evidence, review, governance and replayable accountability.
- Who it is for: enterprise operations, hiring, trust and safety, governance, compliance and AI workflow owners.
- Why now: synthetic identity risk, AI-assisted workflows and fragmented approvals are outpacing traditional review records.
- What is different: Cyber Sentinels connects evidence chains, human governance, verification receipts and replayable timelines into one calm workflow.

## TracFace positioning

TracFace should not be positioned as the primary external platform brand.

Approved positioning:

- Internal identity layer
- Trust protocol
- Verification engine
- Optional trust framework
- Operational identity context

Public references should use calmer terms such as identity layer, trust layer, verification framework or operational identity context. Avoid leading with TracFace in enterprise-facing pages unless the context is technical, internal or explicitly about the verification layer.

## Trust philosophy

Cyber Sentinels is not a lie detector, biometric truth engine or autonomous trust authority.

Trust is treated as an operational workflow:

- Evidence is collected and linked.
- Signals provide context.
- Governance actions remain human-led.
- Receipts explain what was reviewed.
- Timelines preserve operational memory.
- Replay helps teams understand how a workflow evolved.

AI may assist with summaries or gap-finding, but humans remain accountable for decisions, escalations and approvals.

## Operational positioning

The platform should feel calm, enterprise-safe, explainable and operational.

Preferred language:

- Operational Trust Infrastructure
- Hiring Security
- Interview Integrity
- Enterprise Governance
- Verification Workflows
- Evidence Chains
- Verification Receipts
- Replayable Trust Timelines
- Human-governed review

Reduce emphasis on:

- Deepfake detection as a primary claim
- Facial tracking language
- Autonomous AI judgment
- Alarm-heavy security phrasing
- Cyberpunk or threat-dashboard aesthetics

## Enterprise wedge strategy

Hiring Security is the clearest near-term wedge. It should connect candidate provenance, recruiter verification, interview integrity, evidence chains, human governance and replayable review.

Operational Trust is the broader enterprise wedge. It should show how Cyber Sentinels turns fragmented approvals, evidence and accountability into a reviewable workflow.

AI Agent Governance is an expansion wedge. It should focus on agent identity context, ownership, permissions, evidence, human review and replay. Avoid implying that AI agents receive unchecked autonomous authority.

## Pilot conversion path

Pilot onboarding should optimize for a first successful workflow within 10 minutes:

1. Request access
2. Create workspace
3. Upload evidence
4. Trigger governance
5. Generate verification receipt
6. Review replay

Every pilot-facing page should help an enterprise user find the next action without needing to understand the full platform surface.

## Enterprise trust signals

Lightweight trust indicators should remain visible across enterprise and founder-facing surfaces:

- Deployment readiness
- Operational readiness
- Governance philosophy
- Explainability principles
- Human review principles

These signals increase confidence without adding speculative architecture.

## Deferred branding considerations

The existing broader trust ecosystem contains legacy and exploratory terms such as Reality Passport, Human Presence, deepfake detection and agent passport. These can remain in deeper product routes where they already exist, but they should not lead the primary enterprise story.

Future cleanup can consolidate legacy terminology into a smaller public route set once customer learning clarifies which surfaces are still needed. Until then, public navigation and enterprise demos should privilege Cyber Sentinels, Operational Trust Infrastructure, Hiring Security, Enterprise Governance, Evidence Chains and Replayable Trust Timelines.
