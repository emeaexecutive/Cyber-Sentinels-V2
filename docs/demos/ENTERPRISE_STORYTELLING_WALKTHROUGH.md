# Five-Minute Enterprise Walkthrough

## 0:00 - What Cyber Sentinels is

Open `/`. State: Cyber Sentinels is Operational Trust Infrastructure for intelligent enterprises.

## 0:30 - Why Operational Trust matters

Use the comparison panel. Identity, authentication and MFA establish access. Operational Trust continues through authority, runtime control, policy, evidence and governance.

## 1:15 - How the Trust Fabric works

Follow the lifecycle, then One Click Trust. One action collects multiple evidence sources and produces one explainable decision. Use the architecture visual to show that all actor types and workflows share the same Trust Fabric.

## 2:15 - How Replay works

Open `/trust`. Follow the Operational Trust Graph to Replay. Explain that Replay reconstructs material state changes, authority and decisions; it is not merely a video or event list.

## 3:00 - Why Trust Memory™ matters

Follow the timeline from High Trust through risk, authority change, review and restoration. Trust Memory preserves why trust changed and who governed the outcome.

## 3:45 - How providers plug in

Use Provider Transparency. Providers contribute normalized evidence with source, model, version, latency and limitations. Unavailable providers contribute no confidence.

## 4:30 - Why the platform is provider-neutral

Open `/platform`. Show that Provider Orchestrator is one mechanism inside a larger contract. Authority, policy and enforcement remain external to providers and actor runtimes.

Close on `/enterprise`: choose one consequential workflow, agree evidence and authority, validate it in a pilot, then review production gates.
