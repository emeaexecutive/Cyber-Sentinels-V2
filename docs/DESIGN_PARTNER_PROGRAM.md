# Design Partner Program

Cyber Sentinels is prepared for controlled enterprise outreach, pilot onboarding and design partner discussions. The program is focused on practical operational trust: hiring security, session integrity, governance review, replay evidence and verification receipts.

## Ideal Pilot Customers

Ideal design partners have real review pressure and clear workflow ownership.

Good fit customers include:

- enterprise hiring teams facing fake applicants, proxy interviews or stolen identity risk;
- security teams reviewing remote access, high-risk onboarding or sensitive workflow entry;
- talent, risk or compliance teams that need audit-ready evidence around hiring decisions;
- organizations already testing AI-assisted workflows and needing human governance records;
- teams willing to share feedback on review language, receipt usefulness and replay clarity.

Less ideal customers are looking for a generic AI detector, opaque scoring, surveillance tooling or fully automated hiring decisions.

## Onboarding Process

1. Request pilot or design partner access through `/enterprise-access`.
2. Hold a short intro call to confirm scope, workflow owner and data boundary.
3. Start with `/demo`, `/demo/hiring-attack` and `/demo/session-integrity` so stakeholders understand the narrative.
4. Use `/pilot/welcome` and `/pilot/getting-started` for onboarding language and support expectations.
5. Create an isolated pilot workspace through `/enterprise/pilot-setup` only when the pilot is approved.
6. Upload evidence, review flags, open governance review and produce a receipt or replay artifact.

## Governance Workflows

Governance review is the human accountability layer. It should be used when:

- a session integrity signal needs review;
- liveness, deepfake risk or injection risk requires separation from identity confidence;
- evidence is missing or conflicting;
- a reviewer must request more information;
- the workflow needs escalation, approval, rejection or a documented pending state.

Cyber Sentinels should explain governance review as a workflow record, not as AI deciding trust.

## Trust Review Process

The trust review process is intentionally simple:

1. Open the workflow.
2. Review identity and evidence context.
3. Inspect session integrity and risk signals.
4. Record governance review or escalation.
5. Replay the timeline.
6. Export the verification receipt.

The output should help enterprise stakeholders answer: what happened, what evidence existed, who reviewed it, what remains pending and how the workflow can be replayed.

## Enterprise Proof Points

Design partner conversations should highlight:

- operational auditability;
- replay timelines;
- verification receipts;
- governance chronology;
- session integrity review;
- evidence summaries;
- admin readiness views.

Practical hiring language matters. Use fake applicants, proxy interviews, stolen identities, AI-assisted fraud and injected sessions. Avoid vague AI jargon or abstract trust language when a concrete workflow example is available.

## Support Expectations

Pilot support should stay operational and evidence-driven.

For each support issue, capture:

- organization or workspace;
- route involved;
- subject, receipt or replay link;
- expected result;
- actual result;
- whether governance review is pending;
- whether the issue blocks a live walkthrough.

Use `/help`, `/enterprise-access`, `/admin/pilot-overview`, `/admin/runtime-validation` and `/admin/deployment-readiness` as the practical support path before a live demo.

## Roadmap Visibility

Design partners should see what is live, what is partial and what is staged.

Live or pilot-ready:

- Hiring Security narrative;
- Session Integrity walkthrough;
- governance review surfaces;
- replay workflows;
- verification receipts;
- pilot onboarding pages;
- lightweight pilot admin overview.

Staged or optional:

- provider-specific proof-of-human integrations;
- Hopae, World ID, Stripe, OpenAI and email delivery depending on pilot scope;
- broader provider abstraction;
- larger multi-tenant pilot lifecycle management.

The founder narrative stays concise: Trust changed quietly. AI changed identity risk. Verification alone is insufficient. Operational trust infrastructure is emerging for teams that need evidence, review, receipts and replay.
