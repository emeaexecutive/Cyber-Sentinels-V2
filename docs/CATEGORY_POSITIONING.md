# Category Positioning

## Category

Cyber Sentinels is the Operational Trust Infrastructure company for intelligent enterprises.

Operational Trust Infrastructure continuously verifies humans, AI agents, machine identities and regulated workflows before, during and after critical actions. It connects identity, authority, runtime evidence, policy, decision, Replay, Trust Memory™ and accountable governance.

Enterprise Trust Fabric™ remains the internal architecture that coordinates these responsibilities. It is not a second public product category.

## Why the category exists

Identity and access systems establish who may enter. Intelligent enterprise workflows also need to know whether a specific action remains authorized, supported by evidence, explainable and reviewable as context changes. Cyber Sentinels governs that operational interval.

## What Cyber Sentinels is not

- Not a replacement for IAM, IdP, SIEM, GRC or systems of record.
- Not a single detection vendor or biometric verdict.
- Not autonomous social scoring or an opaque trust score.
- Not a claim that configured providers are healthy or accurate.
- Not regulatory certification.

## Differentiation

1. Before, during and after: trust is evaluated across the action lifecycle.
2. Human and machine: one contract supports humans, AI agents and machine identities.
3. Authority plus evidence: identity alone is insufficient for consequential actions.
4. Replayable accountability: decisions retain evidence, policy, authority and governance context.
5. Replaceable providers: adapters preserve source and limitations without vendor lock-in.
6. Trust Memory™: governed outcomes inform future context without autonomously changing policy.
