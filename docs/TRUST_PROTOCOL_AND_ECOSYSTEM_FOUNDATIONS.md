# Trust Protocol and Ecosystem Foundations

## Purpose

Cyber Sentinels creates portable operational trust evidence across enterprise workflows and intelligent systems.

The ecosystem direction is practical interoperability: consistent evidence, replay, governance and receipt semantics that can move with a workflow across enterprise systems. It is not a blockchain, decentralized network or claim of immutable truth.

## Portable trust evidence philosophy

A portable verification receipt is a controlled summary of a workflow outcome. It gives an authorized reader enough context to understand and verify continuity without exposing every underlying sensitive record.

The versioned portable summary contains:

- receipt identifier
- workflow reference
- replay reference
- provider evidence summary
- trust posture snapshot
- governance outcome
- authorization lineage summary
- issuance timestamp

Portability means stable semantics and resolvable references. It does not mean making trust data public.

## Receipt verification

Receipt verification uses deterministic linked-record checks:

1. **Receipt record integrity** — required identity, workflow, time and outcome fields are present.
2. **Replay linkage** — replay or timeline records resolve to the same workflow subject.
3. **Workflow chronology** — timeline events and evidence chains resolve to the receipt workflow.
4. **Governance attribution** — linked governance intervention retains reviewer attribution when governance action exists.

These checks verify operational continuity. They are not cryptographic signatures, immutable-ledger proofs or perfect authenticity guarantees.

## Replayable operational trust

Replay is the canonical operational evidence chain and workflow reconstruction layer. It connects:

- workflow entry and state changes
- provider-backed verification evidence
- session-integrity signals
- evidence additions
- authorization transitions
- governance actions and reviewer attribution
- verification receipt and workflow outcome

Replay remains read-only and access-controlled. Receipts link into replay; replay links back to the receipt and underlying governance chronology.

## Interoperable workflow trust

The lightweight interoperability foundation uses stable application-level structures rather than a new integration platform:

- normalized provider signals
- versioned portable receipt summaries
- workflow and replay references
- evidence-chain references
- explicit governance outcomes
- authorization lineage summaries
- deterministic continuity checks

These structures can support future exports and enterprise integrations without binding trust continuity to one provider or inventing a universal score.

## Provider interoperability

Provider adapters normalize verification state, identity-confidence context, session-integrity context, risk flags and evidence references.

Provider outputs remain attributed to their source. Pending, failed, missing and configured states remain visible. Governance determines the workflow outcome; a provider result does not become automatic truth.

## Governance continuity

Portable evidence must retain the authority behind the outcome:

- who owned the review
- why escalation occurred
- which evidence informed the decision
- how authorization changed
- what trust-state transition resulted
- where the action appears in replay

Governance continuity prevents a receipt from becoming a context-free badge.

## Ecosystem direction

The ecosystem can evolve toward:

- reusable evidence packages
- provider-neutral workflow summaries
- consistent receipt exports
- cross-system replay references
- governance handoffs between authorized enterprise systems

This direction should be driven by real pilot requirements. It does not require blockchain infrastructure, speculative autonomous networks or premature integration breadth.

## Enterprise UX principles

- Receipts should be printable and readable.
- Verification state should be explicit.
- Evidence should appear before interpretation.
- Replay and workflow references should resolve clearly.
- Governance attribution should not be buried.
- Missing continuity should display as review required, not silently pass.
- Portable summaries should avoid sensitive raw evidence.

## Ethical boundaries

- No public exposure of sensitive workflow evidence.
- No surveillance or hidden behavioral tracking.
- No universal identity or reputation score.
- No perfect real/fake detection claim.
- No blockchain or immutable-truth positioning.
- No weakened authentication, admin protection or row-level security.
- No provider result treated as final authority.

Operational trust evidence remains purpose-bound, access-controlled, reviewable and subject to enterprise retention policy.
