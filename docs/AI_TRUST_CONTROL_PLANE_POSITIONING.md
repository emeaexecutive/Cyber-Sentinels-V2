# AI Trust Control Plane Positioning

Last reviewed: 2026-07-06

## Category line

**Trust infrastructure for humans, AI agents and regulated workflows.**

Cyber Sentinels is a decision-layer trust control plane. It keeps identity, Session Integrity, Authorization Lineage, Evidence Chain, Governance Review, Replay Timeline and Persistent Trust Posture connected as consequential work changes. It does not replace systems of record, accountable decision-makers or specialist detection providers.

## What the platform controls

- **Actors:** human, AI-agent, service-account and API identities remain attributable to an owner and purpose.
- **Authority:** delegated scope, approvals, constraints, changes and revocations remain reviewable.
- **Evidence:** provider and workflow evidence retain source, time, confidence and limitations.
- **Decisions:** multi-signal scoring routes accountable review rather than producing a binary truth verdict.
- **Runtime:** material integrity, provenance, behavioral and authorization changes can interrupt reliance.
- **Memory:** Replay reconstructs who or what acted, under whose authority, what evidence existed, why trust changed, what governance occurred and the operational outcome.
- **Sovereignty:** classification, redaction, provider policy and customer-owned operational memory remain stable across provider changes.

## Existing product surfaces

The control-plane model is expressed through existing routes: `/platform` for the operating model; `/trust` and `/trust-timeline/[id]` for trust evolution; `/evidence-vault` and receipt/report surfaces for evidence continuity; `/dashboard/session-integrity` and `/dashboard/governance` for protected review; `/agents` and `/agents/[id]` for governed non-human identities; `/replay/[id]` for canonical operational memory; and `/trust/data-sovereignty` for AI-provider controls. Named concepts do not require duplicate routes.

## Detection and ML boundary

Real ML, Provider API evidence, Heuristic Baseline, Demo Data and Awaiting Credentials are separate states. Baseline scoring is explainable weighted inference labelled `baseline_model_assisted`; it is not trained enterprise AI detection. No fake/authentic conclusion or accuracy claim is valid without representative labelled validation and source-specific benchmark evidence.

## Regulated workflow posture

For fintech, insurance, banking, healthcare, onboarding, claims, approvals and hiring, Cyber Sentinels provides operational traceability: evidence preservation, restricted-data enforcement, human review, reviewer attribution, correction paths, audit exports and governance continuity. Hiring Security is one high-value enterprise access-security workflow, not the whole platform.

## Language guardrails

Lead with governance, replay, evidence continuity, provider control, runtime accountability, workflow integrity and enterprise AI sovereignty. Avoid “magic detection,” autonomous certainty, generic cyber-threat claims and standalone deepfake promises.
