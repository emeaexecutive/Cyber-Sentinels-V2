# Epic 02 Investor Pack

Last updated: 2026-07-08

## Executive Summary

Cyber Sentinels is an enterprise AI Trust Infrastructure platform that makes consequential activity by humans, AI agents, machine identities and regulated workflows explainable, reviewable and replayable.

## Architecture Overview

The platform is organized around five engines: Trust Engine, Runtime Engine, Replay Engine, Governance Engine and Validation Engine. Provider signals are normalized into evidence, not treated as standalone certainty.

## Technical Moat

- Replayable enterprise memory across humans, agents, machine identities and workflows.
- Provider-agnostic trust orchestration with explicit state boundaries.
- Governance-first review and decision history.
- Validation harness that separates implemented, measured, planned and awaiting-data maturity.
- AI sovereignty framing: customer-owned memory and provider policy remain under enterprise control.

## Competitive Positioning

Cyber Sentinels is not a point detector, ATS feature, identity widget or model wrapper. It is the governed trust record across systems, providers and workflows.

## Validation Status

Validation dashboards, benchmark dashboards and provider comparison are implemented. Publishable precision, recall and calibration claims are awaiting reviewed ground-truth datasets from design-partner workflows.

## Provider Roadmap

1. Identity providers.
2. Media provenance and C2PA.
3. Deepfake/media forensics.
4. ATS evidence export.
5. SSO and enterprise identity.
6. Device/session risk.

## Security Posture

Admin/internal routes are protected. Provider credentials are server-side. RLS and MFA/SSO hardening remain production priorities. Public claims must keep capability boundaries explicit.
