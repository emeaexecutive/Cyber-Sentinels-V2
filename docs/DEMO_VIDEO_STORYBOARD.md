# Cyber Sentinels Demo Video Storyboard

## Visual direction

Use calm cuts, restrained motion and readable close-ups. Prefer a single purposeful cursor movement per scene. Let operational states and evidence do the storytelling; avoid dramatic threat graphics, glitch effects or surveillance imagery.

## Scene sequence

### Scene 1 — Homepage | 0:00-0:10

- **Route:** `/`
- **Frame:** Hiring-security headline, short supporting copy and **View Demo** action.
- **Action:** Hold for two seconds, then open the demo.
- **Narrative:** AI changed the conditions of trust in critical workflows.
- **On-screen callout:** `Trust now requires evidence, governance and operational memory.`

### Scene 2 — Hiring attack demo | 0:10-0:20

- **Route:** `/demo/hiring-attack`
- **Frame:** Nine-step operational sequence with **Synthetic candidate enters** selected.
- **Action:** Advance to verification initiation.
- **Narrative:** A synthetic applicant can appear credible while provenance remains incomplete.
- **On-screen callout:** `Synthetic candidate / verification initiated`

### Scene 3 — Session integrity | 0:20-0:34

- **Route:** `/demo/session-integrity`
- **Frame:** Identity state linked, then channel anomaly and injection-risk steps.
- **Action:** Use two clean cuts instead of rapidly clicking through every state.
- **Narrative:** Trust can change after identity verification; channel evidence must remain reviewable.
- **On-screen callout:** `Injection risk elevated`

### Scene 4 — Active flags | 0:34-0:40

- **Route:** `/dashboard/interview-risk`
- **Frame:** Injection-risk flag, session context and review requirement.
- **Action:** Pause on the explanation and evidence source.
- **Narrative:** A flag is operational context, not an automated verdict.
- **On-screen callout:** `Evidence-linked flag / human review required`

### Scene 5 — Governance review | 0:40-0:52

- **Route:** `/governance`
- **Frame:** Open review, assigned reviewer and available governance action.
- **Action:** Show ownership and resolution notes; do not perform a live irreversible action while recording.
- **Narrative:** Governance makes responsibility visible and keeps the decision with a human reviewer.
- **On-screen callout:** `Review opened / accountable owner assigned`

### Scene 6 — Replay timeline | 0:52-1:08

- **Route:** `/replay/[id]`
- **Frame:** Verification chronology joining timeline events, integrity signals, evidence and governance actions.
- **Action:** Scroll once from the summary into the ordered chronology.
- **Narrative:** The audit trail reconstructs what happened without rewriting history.
- **On-screen callout:** `Read-only operational replay`

### Scene 7 — Verification receipt | 1:08-1:22

- **Route:** `/verification/receipt/[id]`
- **Frame:** Identity state, session-integrity state, deepfake risk, injection risk, governance outcome and evidence summary.
- **Action:** Pause on audit references and **Print / Save PDF**.
- **Narrative:** The outcome becomes portable across security, talent and compliance teams.
- **On-screen callout:** `Explainable verification receipt issued`

### Closing frame | 1:22-1:30

- **Frame:** Cyber Sentinels wordmark on a quiet dark background.
- **Primary line:** `The future of AI is not only intelligence. It is trust.`
- **Secondary line:** `Operational Trust Infrastructure for AI-era workflows.`

## Capture checklist

- Seed and verify one sample hiring workflow before recording.
- Confirm replay and receipt IDs in advance; keep them in a private production sheet.
- Close unrelated tabs and disable desktop notifications.
- Record at 1080p or higher with browser zoom optimized for text readability.
- Capture a clean take of every scene plus five seconds of static handles at each end.
- Verify that no real candidate, customer or credential data appears.
