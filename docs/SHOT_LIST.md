For every capture:

- Hide bookmarks, extensions, identifiers and environment details.
- Use one deliberate cursor action at most.
- Record ten-second handles before and after.
- Capture a cursor-free compositing plate.
- Verify readability at final delivery scale.
- Use fictional sample data only.

## Founder Shoot

- Medium close-up, eye level, 65mm equivalent.
- Line: "This is operational trust: evidence, governance and memory for AI-era workflows."
- Record neutral, warmer and slightly firmer versions.
- Hold eye contact and silence five seconds before and after.
- Capture B-roll reviewing replay, reading a receipt and a quiet office wide.

### Lighting

- Large diffused key 45 degrees from camera.
- Negative fill opposite for natural shape.
- Warm practical in depth; cool ambient separation.
- Keep monitor exposure below clipping and skin natural.

## Motion And Overlay Specification

- Slow, linear, motivated camera movement.
- UI animation: 250-350ms ease-out; eight-pixel maximum translation.
- Timeline: one event every 700ms; no looping pulse.
- Risk state: neutral to amber once, then hold.
- Thin restrained cyan evidence connectors; no particles.
- Straight cuts, short dissolves and one timeline-to-receipt match.
- Rebuild overlays from approved product states, not generated text.

## Audio Plan

- Restrained electronic documentary bed, 70-85 BPM.
- Room tone, subtle UI ticks and one low source-change pulse.
- Sound effects at least 12dB below dialogue.
- Music opens under founder line and resolves under end card.
- Record voice in treated space with safety track at -10dB.

## Edit Checklist

- Opening line lands in first second.
- Synthetic hiring risk is clear by 0:14.
- Session integrity precedes governance escalation.
- Human ownership appears before resolution.
- Replay is readable and chronological.
- Receipt includes evidence, reviewer and timestamp.
- Closing line remains verbatim.
- No real candidate, customer, credential or environment data.
- No automated rejection, perfect-detection or surveillance implication.
- End card holds two seconds after final word.
