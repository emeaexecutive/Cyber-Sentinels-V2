# Cyber Sentinels Video Scene Prompts

## Shared Visual Language

Append to every generation:

`cinematic enterprise documentary, photorealistic, restrained near-future technology, charcoal and neutral environment with cyan only for operational status, realistic skin and screen light, soft practical lighting, controlled contrast, subtle film grain, physically plausible reflections, calm composition, no cyberpunk neon, no hooded hackers, no glitches, no floating holograms, no illegible text, no logos, no watermarks`

Generate clean plates without critical typography. Add exact UI labels in post.

## Platform Optimization

### Runway

- Prefer image-to-video for product UI and founder shots.
- Prompt one camera action and one subject action per clip.
- Use 5-8 second clips with low-to-medium motion strength.
- Lock seed and reference frame across adjacent shots.

### Kling

- Order prompts as subject, action, environment, camera and light.
- Use professional mode, 16:9, 5-10 seconds and low creativity.
- Use start/end frames for timeline-to-receipt continuity.

### Veo

- Specify duration, lens, camera movement, ambience and sound intention.
- Request temporal consistency and realistic screen reflections.
- Generate without dialogue except a dedicated founder sync take.

### HeyGen

- Use only for a consented founder avatar or approved presenter.
- Set direct-to-camera delivery, low gesture intensity and 0.95x pacing.
- Do not generate interview participants or imply an avatar is a real candidate.

## Motion Prompt Pack

- Slow two-to-four percent push over six seconds; no acceleration.
- Operational overlays fade and translate eight pixels with 300ms ease-out.
- Status changes animate once, then hold for readability.
- Evidence connectors draw in chronological order without pulsing.
- Replay advances at constant pace with one highlighted event.
- Receipt sections resolve from source evidence, then remain static.
- Cursor movement: one destination, one click, no circling.

## Generation QA

- Reject malformed faces, hands, laptops or screen geometry.
- Reject clips that make a risk signal look like proof of guilt.
- Replace generated UI text with approved product captures.
- Match color, focal length, grain and movement speed across footage.
- Confirm all people are fictional, licensed or explicitly consented.
