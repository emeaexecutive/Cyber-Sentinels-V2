# Stage 2 Compression And Demo Believability

## What Was Simplified

Stage 2 compressed the public product story around operational review instead of conceptual breadth. The homepage now explains the practical chain:

- what happened
- what was detected
- what action occurred
- what evidence was created

Primary navigation was reduced to the enterprise workflow path: Platform, Hiring Security, Session Integrity, Governance, Demo, Enterprise Access and Pricing.

The dashboard was simplified around six operational review areas:

- Active Flags
- Pending Reviews
- Session Integrity
- Verification Receipts
- Threat Activity
- Governance Actions

## What Was Deprioritized

The older conceptual routes remain available, but they are no longer emphasized in the homepage or primary navigation. Stage 2 reduces visible weight for:

- reality-os
- reality-chain
- trust-os
- trust-fabric
- synthetic-counterpart
- human-presence-genome
- origin-dna
- reality-twin
- trust-prediction

These concepts are treated as supporting or archival surfaces rather than the core enterprise narrative.

## Primary Demo Routes

The demo narrative now centers on the proof workflow:

- `/demo/hiring-attack`
- `/demo/session-integrity`
- `/replay/[id]`
- `/verification/receipt/[id]`

The demo sequence is:

1. Fake candidate enters workflow
2. Verification begins
3. Session integrity fails
4. Governance review opens
5. Replay evidence is generated
6. Threat is blocked
7. Verification receipt is issued

## Operational Trust Positioning

Traditional security protects devices, credentials and messages.

Cyber Sentinels protects identity trust, workflow integrity, session authenticity and operational accountability.

The comparison framing is intentionally subtle:

> Existing cybersecurity tools detect threats. Cyber Sentinels verifies operational trust.

## Final Enterprise Narrative Direction

Cyber Sentinels should feel like a calm operational trust platform for enterprise review teams. The product should lead with believable workflows:

- a synthetic applicant enters a hiring process
- an injected interview signal appears
- a reviewer receives evidence and ownership
- replay makes the chronology readable
- a receipt preserves the outcome

The narrative direction is enterprise confidence, not abstract trust theory. The platform should make high-risk workflow changes understandable, reviewable and accountable.
