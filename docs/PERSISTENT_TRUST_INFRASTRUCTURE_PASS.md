# Persistent Trust Infrastructure Pass

## Platform repositioning

Cyber Sentinels is persistent Operational Trust infrastructure for humans, AI agents and enterprise workflows.

The platform connects identity, authorization, evidence, workflow integrity and accountable governance over time. Hiring Security remains an important operational workflow, but it is one application of the platform rather than the company’s entire identity.

## Persistent trust philosophy

Verification is a point-in-time event. Trust is an evolving operational posture.

A trustworthy workflow must be able to explain:

- who or what entered;
- what identity and evidence were available;
- which authorization applied;
- what changed during execution;
- whether Trust Posture shifted;
- where Governance Review intervened;
- what final outcome was recorded; and
- how that chronology can be replayed.

Persistent trust is not a universal score, permanent badge or hidden surveillance system. It is an explainable state derived from retained enterprise workflow records.

## AI agent governance direction

AI agents are first-class operational actors inside the trust platform.

Agent governance connects:

- registered agent identity and accountable ownership;
- declared purpose and permission scope;
- Authorization Lineage;
- policy and revocation state;
- governed execution;
- attributable activity evidence;
- agent Trust Posture; and
- Replay Timeline and audit continuity.

The platform does not claim unrestricted runtime control or autonomous certainty. Human and organizational accountability remains authoritative.

## Continuous identity direction

Continuous Verification applies to human and machine identities after initial access is granted.

Trust Posture can shift when:

- evidence becomes stale or incomplete;
- identity or ownership context changes;
- Session Integrity changes;
- authorization is granted, narrowed or revoked;
- a workflow crosses a governance threshold; or
- a reviewer records an intervention.

Workforce and enterprise identity views should show these shifts as reviewable checkpoints, not automated identity judgments.

## Replay and governance strategy

Replay Timeline is the platform-wide operational memory layer.

Replay supports:

- humans and workforce identities;
- AI agents;
- enterprise workflows;
- authorization changes;
- Governance Review actions;
- Evidence Chain continuity;
- Session Integrity events;
- Trust Posture transitions; and
- final receipts and outcomes.

Replay is read-only reconstruction. It preserves the chronology available at a point in time without rewriting source evidence or governance history.

Governance memory keeps reviewer ownership, escalation rationale, authorization context and resolution attached to the workflow transition they affected.

## Operational trust narrative

The standard platform narrative is:

1. A human, AI agent or workflow enters with declared identity and operating context.
2. Continuous Verification and Evidence Chain establish the available trust basis.
3. Authorization Lineage defines what action is permitted and by whose authority.
4. Trust Posture evolves as evidence, context and execution change.
5. Governance Review intervenes when thresholds or responsibilities require human authority.
6. Replay Timeline reconstructs the complete operational chronology.
7. A receipt preserves the reviewable outcome.

## Standard product language

Use these labels consistently:

- Operational Trust
- Trust Posture
- Continuous Verification
- Evidence Chain
- Authorization Lineage
- Governance Review
- Replay Timeline
- Session Integrity
- governed execution
- governance continuity

Hiring Security should be described as one major Operational Trust workflow.

Avoid candidate-only positioning, isolated fraud-tool framing, unsupported deepfake claims, abstract futuristic control language and disconnected trust sub-brands.

## Security boundary

This pass changes existing positioning, navigation and workflow explanations only. Authentication, admin authorization and row-level security remain unchanged. Protected Trust Center, Trust Posture, Identity Governance, Governance Review, Replay Timeline and enterprise records retain their existing access controls.
