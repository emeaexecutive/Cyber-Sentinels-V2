# ORI Limitations

- ORI defaults off and has no enforcement mode.
- Coefficients are controlled placeholders, not production-trained or cohort-calibrated parameters.
- The eight fixtures are synthetic and establish behavior only.
- No eligible reviewed cohort supports precision, recall, error-rate, agreement, fairness, or calibration claims.
- The model does not verify identity, authority, authenticity, or human presence.
- ORI cannot authorize, block, approve, reject, or replace human governance.
- Seven features are implemented; candidates without reliable normalized evidence are excluded.
- Process-local telemetry is not fleet observability or an SLA.
- Source-level RLS tests do not replace deployed tenant A/B denial evidence.
- Migration application, retention jobs, model-state operations, and target-environment rollback remain deployment work.
- Advisory mode exists as non-enforcing decision support only and is not approved as an enforcement mechanism.
- Hopae, Turnstile, and media-authenticity provider work is outside this sprint.
