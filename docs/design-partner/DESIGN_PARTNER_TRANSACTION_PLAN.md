# Design-partner transaction plan

## Objective

Create the smallest complete design-partner release that preserves the route and packaging shape while giving a partner a credible path to evaluate the platform.

## Transaction flow

1. Partner requests access through the design-partner or enterprise-access route.
2. The experience presents a consultation-based entry point and a bounded proof path.
3. The partner reviews the platform narrative and the evidence-backed release posture.
4. The partner can request a staged pilot or deeper review without the public site making unsupported production claims.

## Success criteria

- The partner journey remains intact without public pricing.
- The partner sees clear boundaries around what is currently implemented versus what is planned.
- The release remains small, reviewable, and safe for staging.

## Non-goals

- Broad public feature expansion.
- New production billing behavior.
- Unsupported autonomous or deepfake claims.
