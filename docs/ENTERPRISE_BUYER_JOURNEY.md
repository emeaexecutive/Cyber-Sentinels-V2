# Enterprise Buyer Journey

The public experience is designed to answer the buyer's core questions in under three minutes, then support a five-minute guided walkthrough.

| Moment | Buyer question | Product surface | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:30 | What is this? | Homepage hero | Operational trust control plane positioning |
| 0:30-1:00 | Why is identity not enough? | Comparison panel | Access moment versus operational lifecycle |
| 1:00-1:45 | How does trust work? | Lifecycle and One Click Trust | Identity through Continuous Trust |
| 1:45-2:15 | What is the architecture? | Trust Fabric visual | Actors and workflows to accountable outcomes |
| 2:15-3:00 | Why is it different? | Trust Memory and Operational Trust Graph | Evolving, replayable trust |
| 3:00-4:00 | Can we inspect the proof? | Trust Center | Replay, evidence, providers, validation and sovereignty |
| 4:00-5:00 | How do we start? | Solutions and Enterprise | One workflow, one pilot, controlled production gates |

The journey uses one primary CTA per page. Detailed concepts are linked to their canonical owner rather than re-explained.
