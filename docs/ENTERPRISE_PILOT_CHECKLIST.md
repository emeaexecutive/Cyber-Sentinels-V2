# Enterprise Pilot Checklist

## Before kickoff

- Name the customer sponsor, pilot owner, security reviewer, operational reviewer and Cyber Sentinels pilot owner.
- Select one consequential workflow and document the authority boundary.
- Approve sample data, retention, provider egress and restricted-data controls.
- Agree measurable success criteria and rollback triggers.

## Deployment timeline

| Phase | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Week 0 | Scope, owners, security boundary and rollback triggers approved |
| Week 1 | Workspace, data mapping and provider readiness reviewed |
| Week 2 | Allow, review and block paths exercised with evidence capture |
| Week 3 | Outcomes reviewed and production-readiness decision recorded |

## Success metrics

- Every reviewed decision links rationale, authority, evidence, Replay and owner.
- Every escalation has a named reviewer, disposition and timestamp.
- JSON, PDF and Enterprise Summary Trust Evidence Packs can be generated for approved cases.
- Customer reviewers can locate posture, evidence, next action and limitations without assistance.
- No unresolved critical-path failure remains at closeout.

## Responsibilities and support

The customer supplies accountable owners, approved data, access boundaries, reviewers and outcome adjudication. Cyber Sentinels configures the bounded workflow, preserves evidence boundaries and documents limitations. Support contacts are named roles agreed at kickoff; an unassigned mailbox is not treated as operational ownership.

## Rollback

Pause new submissions, preserve audit records, disable the affected integration without weakening authorization, restore the documented pre-pilot decision path, notify named owners, export retained evidence and require approval before restart.
