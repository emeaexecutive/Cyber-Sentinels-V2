# Platform Rebalancing Pass

## Platform position

Cyber Sentinels is operational trust infrastructure for humans, AI agents and enterprise workflows.

The platform maintains an explainable trust posture as identity, evidence, authorization and workflow context change. Governance interventions, authorization lineage and replayable evidence preserve why a sensitive action was allowed, restricted or escalated.

Hiring Security remains a major workflow domain. It demonstrates the platform in a high-consequence setting, but it is not the boundary of the platform.

## Core platform story

1. A human, AI agent or workflow enters with declared identity and context.
2. Evidence and authorization establish the current operating boundary.
3. Trust posture evolves as evidence, permissions or workflow context change.
4. Governance assigns accountable human authority to sensitive transitions.
5. Replay reconstructs the evidence, trust change, authorization and intervention.
6. A receipt preserves the reviewable outcome.

## Primary trust surfaces

- **Persistent trust posture** — current state derived from retained workflow evidence and governance history.
- **Continuous identity** — verification freshness and context across the operational lifecycle.
- **AI agent trust** — registered identity, declared purpose, permission scope and attributable activity.
- **Enterprise authorization** — grants, changes and revocations connected to accountable authority.
- **Governance continuity** — named ownership and intervention history across workflow transitions.
- **Replayable evidence** — canonical chronology explaining what changed and why.
- **Governed execution** — sensitive human and agent activity within visible policy and evidence boundaries.

## Navigation hierarchy

- **Platform** — operational trust, posture, agents, replay and governance.
- **Hiring Security** — a major domain applying the platform to hiring workflows.
- **Trust Center** — protected operational posture, evidence and governance continuity.
- **Enterprise** — identity governance, authorization, controls and access.
- **Pricing** — commercial plans.
- **Access** — authenticated product entry.

## Domain framing

### Humans

Continuous identity and consent-based verification across workforce and enterprise workflows. Trust is not a permanent badge or universal score.

### AI agents

Agent identity remains linked to accountable people and organizations. Permission scope, policy status, governed execution and activity evidence remain reviewable.

### Enterprise workflows

Evidence, authorization, governance and outcomes remain connected as work crosses systems and teams.

### Hiring Security

Candidate, recruiter and interview integrity are one major application of the same operational trust infrastructure. The product records observable changes and reviewer action without claiming automatic fraud certainty.

## Language boundaries

Use:

- persistent trust posture
- continuous identity
- operational trust continuity
- AI agent governance
- authorization lineage
- governed execution
- governance continuity
- replayable evidence
- human and machine activity

Avoid:

- presenting Cyber Sentinels primarily as a candidate-fraud product
- implying permanent trust or universal identity scores
- claiming autonomous decisions without accountable human authority
- implying runtime control of agents where only identity, evidence and governance records exist
- unsupported biometric, deepfake or fraud-accuracy claims

## Access and security

This pass changes positioning and navigation only. Existing authentication, admin checks and row-level security remain unchanged. Protected Trust Center, identity-governance, registry and dashboard data stay protected.
