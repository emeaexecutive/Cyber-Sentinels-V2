# Enterprise Narrative

## Category statement

Cyber Sentinels is an enterprise decision platform for governed operational trust. It helps teams decide whether consequential activity by humans, AI agents and machine identities should continue, pause for review or stop.

## Five buyer questions

Every major experience answers:

1. **Why am I here?** A decision or workflow carries material uncertainty.
2. **What is happening?** Identity, authority, runtime risk and evidence have a current explainable state.
3. **What should I do next?** One recommended action is visible.
4. **Who is responsible?** A workflow owner or governance reviewer is named.
5. **Can I prove it?** Evidence, Replay, governance rationale and Trust Memory\u2122 retain the decision history.

Technical names remain valid in developer and architecture documentation. Buyer-facing surfaces lead with the operational outcome.

## Decision journey

`Identity -> Authority -> Runtime -> Decision -> Replay -> Governance -> Trust Memory\u2122 -> Evidence Graph -> Outcome`

The journey is not an autonomous truth pipeline. Evidence and ML can inform the decision; policy, authority and accountable governance determine whether work advances.
