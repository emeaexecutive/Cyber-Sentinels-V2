# Final Founder Pitch

## One-line pitch

Cyber Sentinels is Operational Trust Infrastructure that helps enterprises protect hiring and other sensitive workflows against synthetic identity attacks with verification evidence, session integrity, governance review, replay, and receipts.

## 30-second pitch

AI changed identity risk quietly. It is now easier for a fake applicant, proxy interviewer, injected session, or AI-assisted actor to enter an enterprise workflow looking legitimate. Cyber Sentinels protects those workflows by showing what happened, what changed, what evidence was reviewed, and what decision was made. Hiring Security is the first wedge because the risk is urgent, visible, and expensive. The broader product becomes Operational Trust Infrastructure: a replayable evidence layer for human-governed decisions in high-risk workflows.

## 90-second pitch

Cyber Sentinels helps organizations answer a question that existing tools do not fully cover: can this workflow still be trusted after access begins?

Identity tools verify a person at a point in time. Security tools protect accounts, devices, email, and endpoints. Deepfake tools may classify a piece of media. But modern synthetic identity attacks move across the workflow. A fake candidate can pass an initial screen, use AI assistance in an interview, introduce video or audio mismatch, trigger injection risk, and leave teams with no clean chronology of what happened.

Cyber Sentinels turns that into an operational trust record. It captures verification evidence, Session Integrity signals, Governance Review actions, Replay Evidence, and Verification Receipts. The goal is not to claim perfect detection or replace human judgment. The goal is to give enterprises a calm, audit-ready way to see risk, review evidence, record a decision, and prove what happened later.

We start with Hiring Security because it is one of the clearest AI-era trust failures: fake applicants, proxy interviews, and AI-assisted fraud create immediate business risk. From there, Cyber Sentinels extends into the broader trust layer enterprises will need for agents, contractors, privileged workflows, and other high-stakes operations.

## Why now

- AI makes identity attacks cheaper, more convincing, and easier to scale.
- Remote hiring and distributed work have expanded the surface area for synthetic participation.
- Enterprises are being asked to make decisions from fragmented evidence across video, identity, HR, security, and compliance tools.
- Point-in-time verification is no longer enough when risk can appear during the session.
- Boards, security leaders, legal teams, and talent leaders increasingly need replayable evidence, not only alerts.

## Why existing tools are not enough

Existing categories solve important parts of the problem, but not the whole operational workflow.

- Identity and access tools help decide who gets in, but they do not fully explain what happens during a sensitive workflow.
- Endpoint, email, and cloud security tools protect infrastructure, but they do not create a human-readable trust chronology for hiring or review decisions.
- Deepfake detection can be useful as a signal, but it is not the same as governance, reviewer action, receipt generation, or replayable evidence.
- HR and applicant tracking systems manage process, but they are not designed to evaluate synthetic identity risk across a session.

Cyber Sentinels connects these layers into verification evidence, Session Integrity, Governance Review, Replay Evidence, and Verification Receipts.

## Why Hiring Security is the wedge

Hiring Security is the clearest entry point because the pain is immediate and cross-functional.

- Talent teams see candidate fraud and proxy interviews directly.
- Security teams worry about insider risk and identity spoofing before employment begins.
- Compliance and legal teams need an audit-ready record when a candidate is rejected or escalated.
- Business leaders understand the cost of hiring the wrong person into a trusted role.

The hiring workflow also creates a natural story for demo calls: fake candidate enters, verification begins, Session Integrity fails, Governance Review opens, Replay Evidence appears, reviewer action is recorded, and a Verification Receipt is issued.

## Why Operational Trust Infrastructure is the moat

The moat is not a single detector. The moat is the operational record.

Cyber Sentinels builds durable trust memory across high-risk workflows:

- A clear chronology of verification and session events.
- Evidence labels that separate identity, presence, injection, and governance signals.
- Human reviewer actions tied to specific evidence.
- Receipts that explain the final state without exposing unnecessary internal data.
- Replay that helps teams understand what happened without turning the product into surveillance.

As AI changes how work is performed, organizations will need infrastructure that makes trust reviewable, governable, and repeatable. That is the long-term role of Cyber Sentinels.
